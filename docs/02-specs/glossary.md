# Glossary

The terms used in the problem statements (`01-discovery/`), the specs (`02-specs/`) and
the [policy register](policy-register.md). Every requirement uses a term only in the sense
given here. Where an older document used a different word, the word it replaces is shown
as *replaces …*.

## Conventions for units and time

| Quantity | Convention |
|---|---|
| Rate | Mbps (10⁶ bits per second). Gbps is written only in prose, as 1 Gbps = 1,000 Mbps. |
| Volume of traffic | Decimal units: KB = 10³ bytes, GB = 10⁹ bytes, TB = 10¹² bytes, PB = 10¹⁵ bytes. At 100 Mbps, one minute carries 0.75 GB. |
| Money | An amount always carries its currency, for example `12.50 USD`. Exact amounts are given in **micros** where precision matters: `1 USD = 1,000,000 micro-USD`. |
| Time of day | `HH:MM` or `HH:MM:SS` in UTC on one calendar day, unless a scenario says otherwise. A time stated to the second in a scenario is exact (`09:31:00` is the minute boundary itself, not any instant in that second). A time at which something is **recorded** ("recorded at") is the commit timestamp of its record. |
| Window | Half-open: `[10:00, 11:00)` includes 10:00 and excludes 11:00. |
| Durations | Written with their unit (`30 s`, `24 h`, `12 months`, `14 calendar days`). |

## People and parties

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Account** | The entity that pays or is paid: a buyer account or a supplier account. Reservations, booking requests, the wallet, agent keys and statements belong to an account. *Replaces "customer" where the paying entity is meant.* |
| **Account administrator** | A user of a buyer account with the right to issue, change and revoke the account's agent keys and set their limits. Only an account administrator can do these things, add and remove the account's users and grant or remove their administrator right (an account always keeps at least one administrator), register, change and remove the account's webhook endpoints, and give or withdraw consent to identify the account to a named supplier (POL-33, POL-32); an agent key never can (POL-25). An administrator granted by an **account recovery** is restricted for `recovery_hold` (POL-32). |
| **Supplier administrator** | A user of a supplier account with the right to add and remove the supplier's users, grant or remove their supplier administrator right, manage the supplier's credentials (its exporters' included) and register its webhook endpoints (POL-32). |
| **Account recovery** | Restoring an administrator to a buyer or supplier account none of whose administrators can act: onboarding (Share's know-your-business process) re-verifies the business and grants the administrator right to a user it names, every user of the account being told. For `recovery_hold` an objection by an administrator who held the right before cancels it, and the new administrator is held: it cannot remove users or administrator rights, issue agent keys or the supplier's credentials (exporters' included), register or change webhook endpoints or give consent; a right it grants is held likewise and removed on a cancellation; an erasure request it passes on is held, opened when the recovery is final and refused, its subject told, on a cancellation, its request time unchanged; users added in the hold remain after a cancellation, listed in its notice (POL-32, POL-19). |
| **Buyer** | A person, an enterprise or an AI agent acting for a buyer account. *Replaces "client" and "customer" where the person or agent is meant.* |
| **Agent key** | A credential an account issues to an AI agent or other automated client. It acts only for that account, and carries its own `key_spend_limit` per `key_spend_period`, `key_inflight_limit` and `key_rate_limit` (POL-25). An account has at most `account_max_keys` keys. Revoking a key takes effect for new requests within 60 s; its requests not yet decided then fail with the reason "key revoked" (POL-25, POL-04). |
| **Key spending** | For one agent key in the current `key_spend_period`, counted cumulatively: the charges accrued in the period by the parts the key booked or added (a reservation it booked, an extension or increase it made), whether a hold covered them or not and including parts that have since ended, plus the funds currently held for their future charges. Each minute is counted once; a part another principal booked or added never counts toward the key's spending (POL-25). |
| **Key headroom** | The headroom shown to and enforced for an agent key: the lower of the account's headroom and `key_spend_limit` less key spending (POL-25). It can be negative, when the key's own reservations keep accruing past its limit; a key headroom below what a booking needs blocks new bookings and additions by that key only, and never ends, suspends or reduces a reservation or affects the account's other keys (POL-25). |
| **Agent operator** | The account administrator who issues agent keys and sets their limits. |
| **Principal** | Whoever performs an operation, once authenticated: an account user, an agent key, a supplier user, an exporter, an operator or an internal service. Records identify it by a **pseudonymous identifier**, stable for the retention period, never by personal data (POL-19). |
| **Personal data** | Information that identifies a person, such as a name or contact detail. It is never written to logs, audit records, history or financial records; it is held apart, in a store from which it can be **erased** so that it cannot be recovered, after which the pseudonymous identifiers in other records no longer lead to the person (POL-19). An **erasure** is executed only on a recorded **erasure request**: a user's request for its own data, through an administrator of its account or by a documented request it makes itself; an operator's for its own data, through workforce administration; a **documented erasure request** by or for a person who is not a Share user, naming the exporters and the period concerned, verified by compliance; or, with no request, a documented legal requirement, approved by a second compliance operator and recorded as a request with its legal reference. One operator in compliance executes it, and it is complete in every copy (the live store, every backup and every **escrowed copy**, such as a key held in escrow) within `erasure_max` of the request's record (POL-19, POL-32). |
| **Operator** | A member of Share's staff acting under exactly one of the roles of POL-32: **support** (reads, and acts on an account only at its **documented request**: one made through the marketplace by an authenticated principal of the account who may make that change itself, recorded), **network operations** (records outages and restorations, terminates for network abuse, declares region disasters with a second approval), **commercial** (sets prices and split weights with a second approval, records partner terms, registers and retires links), **finance** (price corrections, quarantine attribution and its reversal, credit limits, `usage_rate_cap`, money escalations) and **compliance** (terminates for fraud or a sanctions or legal order, discloses a buyer's identity to a supplier under a legal order, and withholds a termination notice a legal order forbids, as part of that termination, each with a second compliance operator's approval; erases a data subject's personal data on a recorded erasure request, one operator, verifying a request from a person who is not a Share user first, and opens an erasure with no request only on a documented legal requirement, with a second compliance operator's approval; reads the audit trail). Each operator action, and each read it needs, belongs to exactly one role. An action needing a second approval lapses unapproved after `approval_expiry` (POL-32). |
| **Supplier** | An infrastructure provider whose links are sold through the marketplace and who is paid a share of the charges for them. Only a link's own supplier may book or cancel maintenance on it or change its capacity, increases included (POL-07). |
| **Partner** | A supplier whose link's schedule is kept in the partner's own system, not Share's. A link of this kind is a **partner link**; a link whose schedule Share keeps is a **Share link**. |
| **Partner without holds** | A partner that can only book immediately and finally, and cannot set capacity aside for a time first. A partner whose holds cannot last `route_deadline_max` plus `commit_margin`, or that does not accept an absolute expiry time for a hold set by Share when the hold is taken, is treated as one (POL-26, POL-28). |
| **Payment provider** | The service a buyer pays through (M-Pesa, Stripe, Adyen and similar). It notifies the marketplace of payments by **payment notification**. |
| **Payment partner** | The licensed institution that holds buyer funds for Share (POL-24). |
| **Network operations** | The team and systems that act on the network: police traffic, block it at the edge, provision connections. |

## Capacity and booking

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Link** | A connection with a fixed **capacity** in Mbps that is sold in slices. It is registered for its supplier, with its capacity, and retired, by an operator in commercial; it is retired only once nothing is reserved, held, offered or booked as maintenance on it from then on (POL-07, POL-32). |
| **Capacity in force** | A link's capacity at a minute: the lowest of its **announced capacity** (as registered, changed by its supplier's permanent reductions and increases) and the capacity of every unplanned outage open at that minute. Every capacity change states a level, never an amount; new sales and outage-affected reservations are judged against the capacity in force, while a permanent reduction removes reservations only to fit the announced capacity (POL-07). |
| **Schedule** | Everything that occupies a link's capacity, minute by minute: confirmed reservations and capacity holds. |
| **Schedule owner** | The single authority that decides every booking on a link, single-link or route (POL-05). There is exactly one at a time per link. It is **failing over** from the loss of the current owner until a new one has taken over the link's schedule; meanwhile no release on that link can become durable, so a Share-link hold may be released, and an outcome other than confirmed reported, up to `release_grace` after the decision or the deadline instead (POL-04, POL-26). |
| **Route** | One or more links, in order, booked for the same window at the same reserved rate, as one unit. A booking on a single link is a route of one leg (POL-05). The legs are numbered in route order, first leg first (POL-12). |
| **Leg** | One link of a route. |
| **Reservation** | A confirmed right to carry traffic up to a reserved rate on every leg of a route during a window. A reservation is created, changed and ended as one unit (POL-08). A buyer cannot book different rates for sub-windows (that is a separate reservation); a change of rate applies from the next minute boundary to the end of the window (POL-08), so a reservation's rate can differ before and after a change. |
| **Reservation reference** | The opaque reference, used for no other reservation, by which a supplier sees and provisions a reservation on its link. A supplier sees the reservation's rate, window, **endpoints** (its termination points on that supplier's own link, never the route's ends) and this reference, never the buyer's account identity unless the buyer consents (by an account administrator, per named supplier, withdrawable for the future; a withdrawal cannot unlink what was already seen) or a legal order requires it (disclosed by compliance with a second compliance operator's approval, for the order's scope and until its end) (POL-33). |
| **Part of a reservation** | What one admission added: the original booking, or the difference added by one extension or rate increase (POL-08). Each part keeps its own price and its own age, on an equivalent path it moves to too; a decrease removes the most recently added part first (POL-08, POL-07). |
| **Reserved rate** | The rate, in Mbps, that a reservation guarantees. *Replaces "usage" when used of reserved capacity.* |
| **Committed rate** | On one link at one minute: the sum of the reserved rates of the confirmed reservations on it, plus any capacity held on it for an open equivalent-path offer (POL-07). Planned maintenance is never in it. From the end of a permanent reduction's notice, each reservation counts at the rate the reduction leaves it (POL-07). |
| **Peak** | The highest committed rate, or committed rate plus capacity held, at any minute of a window. |
| **Booking request** | A buyer's request to create or change a reservation. It carries an idempotency key, a deadline and a `wait` setting, and ends with exactly one outcome. |
| **Outcome** | What a booking request ends as: **confirmed** (every leg secured; the reservation exists, or the change is made), **rejected** (it does not fit, or funds, limits or standing do not allow it, or a booking rule of POL-08 or POL-28 does not allow it; the rejection is a counter-offer where one can be computed, and states the rule otherwise), **invalid** (the request itself is malformed; it is neither confirmed nor rejected), or **failed** (it could not be completed for another reason, such as its deadline passing ("deadline passed while waiting" for a waiter, with a `retry_after`), the buyer withdrawing it ("withdrawn") or its agent key being revoked ("key revoked"); it holds nothing). A request not confirmed by its deadline has failed as of its deadline, whatever the state of Share's booking process, and every later status reports that. A request is confirmed only by a decision timestamped before its deadline, and no confirmation is ever decided after the deadline; one decided in time may become known up to `release_grace` after the deadline if the decision record was unavailable at it (POL-26). Each outcome is decided at one point in time; until then a request is **pending**, and whether it was still pending when something else happened (a suspension, a key revocation, a withdrawal, its deadline) is judged against that point (POL-04). *Replaces "admitted", "allowed", "refused" (of a booking request), "fails as an invalid window".* |
| **Not accepted** | A submission that does not become a booking request: refused before anything is decided, because it is unauthenticated, over a rate limit, or reuses an idempotency key for a different request or from a different principal (POL-25, POL-29). No request is created, nothing is held, it has no outcome, and it never changes the request that first used the key. A key that was not already in use may be used again. |
| **Capacity hold** | Capacity set aside on a link for a pending booking request. It counts against the link's capacity. Concurrent requests are checked one at a time in arrival order, each counting the holds then in place, so a request refused because of a hold later released stays refused (POL-05, POL-25). On a link, arrival order is the order in which the requests reach the link's schedule owner (POL-05). A request takes no capacity hold until its funds hold or credit hold is taken (POL-25). On a Share link it is released when the request has an outcome other than confirmed: the release is durable before that outcome is reported, and the capacity is free for other buyers from the moment the outcome is reported (POL-04): within `status_staleness` of the decision, or within `release_grace` of it while the link's schedule owner is failing over. It is released at the latest at the request's deadline; if the request's decision record is unavailable at the deadline, or the link's schedule owner is failing over then, within `release_grace` after it, unless a confirmation decided in time makes it the reservation's (POL-26). At a partner it lapses by itself at the expiry Share set to the deadline when it took the hold, and Share also releases it at the deadline (POL-26). A leg the partner has already confirmed is a partner booking, not a hold: if the request is left without Share's confirmation, Share cancels it at once, at its own cost (POL-28). **Capacity in flight** is the Mbps in requests not yet decided, counted once per request at its reserved rate, whatever the number of legs, and limited per key and per account by `key_inflight_limit` and `account_inflight_limit`, the latter never below `account_inflight_floor` (POL-25). *Replaces "hold" where it meant capacity.* |
| **Funds hold** | Money in a wallet set aside, not charged, to cover a reservation's charges for the coverage window (POL-15), or a partner cancellation fee if larger (POL-08), but only while that fee could still be charged: after the partner's minimum term the hold is the coverage (POL-15). From the minute a reservation ends nothing is held for its later charges; its unposted earlier charges and a fee due stay held until posted (POL-15). At release after suspension, a shown fee is charged against what is left of the hold, and any shortfall becomes a negative balance. It reduces headroom, not the ledger balance, and it is not a money movement. It is taken before any capacity hold, and released at the request's outcome if the request is not confirmed (POL-25). For a reservation that starts in the future it is taken at admission and kept until the reservation starts. It is resized when an open-ended reservation's price changes; the price takes effect even if the larger hold cannot be covered (POL-27). |
| **Credit hold** | For a credit-limit account, the same amount a funds hold would be, taken at admission against the credit limit. It counts as funds held in the headroom formula (POL-15, POL-18). |
| **Coverage window** | The period of future charges that a funds hold covers: `coverage_window` (POL-15), or the whole reservation if shorter. The hold rolls forward as charges accrue, except while the account is suspended. |
| **Deadline** | The time by which a booking request has its outcome. Chosen per request, at least `route_deadline_min`, or `route_deadline_min` + `commit_margin` with a partner leg, and at most `route_deadline_max`; `route_deadline_default` if not stated (POL-26). Every outcome exists by the deadline: a request does not begin committing later than deadline less its completion margin, `commit_margin` with a partner leg and `ack_budget` otherwise, and is confirmed only by a decision before the deadline (POL-26). A request still pending at its deadline raises an operational alert. |
| **Waiting** | A request that fits only if a request in flight ends without confirmation waits, pending, within its own deadline (POL-05). So does a request refused definitively by a partner while another Share request holds, or has booked for a request not yet decided, overlapping capacity at that partner; it asks the partner again when that hold or booking resolves (a partner booking for a request not confirmed resolves when Share has cancelled it) (POL-02, POL-28). The priority rule orders requests that are waiting (POL-02). A buyer can ask not to wait (`wait = false`) and is then rejected at once with `retry_after` and the counter-offer computed beside the capacity in flight. |
| **Crossing** | A younger request that holds capacity an older request is waiting for, and is itself waiting. It releases that capacity and waits within its own deadline, keeping its place among waiters (POL-02). Two requests each holding what the other needs is the pairwise case; the same rule breaks cycles of three or more. A holder that is not waiting is never pre-empted. |
| **Contention guarantee** | Contention alone never makes every competitor lose. It covers requests that wait (the default) and whose deadline, less their completion margin, outlasts the outcome of the request ahead of them, on Share links and on partner links where Share's own holds, or its bookings for requests not yet decided, cause the contention. A request that asks not to wait, or whose deadline, less its completion margin, ends before the outcome it waits on, has excluded itself; it is told why ("deadline passed while waiting", with a `retry_after`) or given the counter-offer. A loss caused by a failure (a partner's refusal not caused by another Share request's hold or undecided booking, a fault, an expired deadline after a fault) is not a contention loss; for it, nothing is left held and the buyer is told why (POL-02). |
| **Completion margin** | The time a request keeps inside its deadline to be confirmed: it does not begin committing later than its deadline less its completion margin, `commit_margin` for a request with a partner leg, `ack_budget` otherwise. Its confirmation still counts if decided at any time before the deadline. It also scopes the contention guarantee, and is not a parameter of its own (POL-02, POL-26, POL-04). |
| **Decision record** | Share's record of a booking request's decision: the one point at which its outcome is decided (POL-04). A confirmation counts only if its entry is timestamped before the request's deadline; none is ever decided after the deadline. It is **unavailable at the deadline** if it is unavailable at it or at any time in the last `ack_budget` before it (POL-26). If the record is unavailable at the deadline, operations is alerted, status reads report pending until it is available again, and by `release_grace` after the deadline at the latest the request is known: confirmed, if a confirmation was decided in time, otherwise failed as of its deadline, its holds on Share links released (POL-26). |
| **Status read** | Any answer that reports a booking request's status: a poll, a retry with its idempotency key, or the like. It may lag the request's decision by at most `status_staleness` (POL-04), reporting pending until it reflects the decision; it never reports an outcome other than the one decided. An outcome other than confirmed is reported only once its Share-link releases are durable, within `status_staleness` of the decision, or within `release_grace` of it while a link's schedule owner is failing over, pending until then. While the request's decision record is unavailable (failing over), before or at its deadline, it reports pending or the decided outcome, never another, even beyond `status_staleness`, until the record is available again: at most admission's recovery time (1 min) after it became unavailable, and at most `release_grace` after the deadline when it is unavailable at the deadline (POL-04, POL-26). Neither applies once the outcome has been reported: every later status read reports it (POL-04). |
| **Told** | A principal has been told something within a time once the notice is recorded in its account's list of notices, readable by a status read, and its first delivery to the account's registered webhook endpoints, if any, has been attempted, both within that time; an endpoint that is down does not make the notice late, and delivery is retried for `webhook_retry` (POL-04). One delivery may carry many notices; each notice it carries has its first delivery attempted when that delivery is first attempted (POL-04). |
| **Withdrawal** | A buyer ending its own pending request. The request fails with the reason "withdrawn" and releases what it holds (POL-08). |
| **`ack_budget`** | The time within which every booking request receives a response, final or pending (POL-04). |
| **`retry_after`** | In the immediate rejection of a request that asked not to wait (`wait = false`) and needs capacity held for another request in flight: that request's remaining deadline, after which the capacity is either confirmed or released (POL-05). Also, in a submission not accepted over a rate limit, when the principal may send again (POL-25). Also, for a waiter failed with "deadline passed while waiting", the remaining deadline of the request ahead of it at that moment, or zero if that request already has its outcome (POL-02). With several blocking requests (on one link or on several legs), the latest of their remaining deadlines, zero for each that already has its outcome (POL-05, POL-02). Also, in the rejection of a maintenance booking blocked only by capacity in flight (POL-07). |
| **Counter-offer** | The content of a rejection that tells the buyer what they can get instead (POL-10). For a route: the largest rate that fits every leg for the requested window, the earliest window of the requested length, starting at or after the requested start and within the selling horizon, that fits the requested rate on every leg (POL-10), and the link that blocked it. Every counter-offer, of a `wait = false` rejection or of a rejection for no fit, is computed beside the capacity committed and the capacity held for requests in flight (POL-10). For insufficient funds it states two amounts: the minimum payment to be confirmed (the hold less the headroom), and that minimum plus `low_balance_warning` (POL-10). |
| **Idempotency key** | A key the buyer chooses for a booking request. It is scoped to the account and identifies its request for `idempotency_retention` (POL-29). The same key from the same principal with the same route, window, rate, product and `wait` setting is the same request; for a change, with the same reservation, kind of change, new end or new rate, and `wait` setting; any other reuse within the retention is not accepted. |
| **Source reference** | The reference by which a money-moving input is identified, and by which it is idempotent (POL-29): a payment notification, an invoice payment, a transfer confirmation or failure report, a payout instruction, an operator correction or quarantine attribution, the resolution of a money escalation, a write-off, the posting of a charge, an amendment or a fee line, a movement for a cost Share bears, a supplier debt, or a movement of Share's own funds. |
| **Window** | The period a reservation covers, `[start, end)`, at minute resolution, or `[start, open)` for an **open-ended** reservation that lasts until cancelled. |
| **Minute boundary** | The start of a whole minute (`HH:MM:00`). |
| **Selling horizon** | How far ahead the marketplace sells: a rolling `selling_horizon` from now (POL-09), in calendar months in UTC, ending on the same date and time of day, or on the last day of the month if it has no such date. For a request, **now** is the minute boundary at or before the moment it was received (POL-09). *Replaces "horizon" and "booking horizon".* |
| **Affected reservation** | On an unplanned outage, a confirmed reservation on the link at each minute, from the start of the minute in which the outage is recorded until the first minute boundary at or after its restoration is recorded, at which the committed rate exceeds the capacity in force: every reservation at those minutes alike, none chosen first. An outage that leaves capacity at or above what is committed affects none; planned maintenance never makes a reservation affected, and from a reduction's notice end each reservation counts at the rate the reduction leaves it (POL-07). Holders active at the record are told within `affected_notice_time`; the holder of a reservation that starts during the outage, within `affected_notice_time` of its start; and holders are told again within `affected_notice_time` whenever the minutes their reservation is affected change (POL-07). Capacity held for an open equivalent-path offer counts in the committed rate but is not flagged (POL-07). On a permanent reduction, only the reservations (or parts) that would otherwise not fit the reduced announced capacity, never more because of an outage open when the notice ends: just enough Mbps, newest part first, each part cut by the largest excess it meets after newer parts' cuts, recomputed as the schedule changes until the notice ends (POL-07). A request whose confirmation was already being decided (it had begun committing) when a reduction was recorded may still be confirmed; it is then affected like any other, and network operations is alerted (POL-07). It had begun committing exactly when the reduction's **displacement record** (the record, made at the link with the reduction, of the requests in flight it no longer fits) is not visible to the transaction confirming it (POL-07); POL-26's bound on when committing begins is a deadline fence only, and does not decide this. |
| **Revocation** | Ending a confirmed reservation by Share's decision under POL-07 (permanent reduction, when no equivalent path is accepted, at the end of `revocation_notice`). |
| **Release** | Ending a suspended account's reservations at the end of `suspension_grace`: a termination for non-payment (POL-16). |
| **Termination** | Ending a reservation on Share's own decision for a reason in POL-30's closed list (a support operator acting at the account's documented request makes the account's own change, POL-08, and never terminates): fraud, a sanctions or legal order, or abuse of the network. It records the reason and the operator; the holder is told unless compliance, with a second approval, records a legal order forbidding it, as part of the termination itself (a later request to withhold is refused); charges stop at termination and charges already accrued are not refunded (POL-30). |
| **Equivalent path** | On a permanent reduction, the path offered to an affected buyer for the whole reservation at its full rate, even when only part of it is affected: the same endpoints and rate, latency and SLA class no worse, at the lower of the original price and the path's price. Only when the path is dearer does Share bear the difference (per minute, the path's charge computed exactly and rounded down once, less the buyer's charge): it is split by Webster among the path's parties, each supplier is paid its share from Share's own account, and the commission share is not posted (POL-23); the path's parties are split on what is charged. If accepted, all of the reservation moves when the notice ends, or earlier from a minute boundary the holder chooses; if declined or lapsed, only the affected Mbps are removed. The offer lapses after `offer_window`; no answer counts as declined; an offer once made stays open even if the reservation is no longer affected; each part keeps its age on the new path; a move that becomes final only after the reduction's cut carries the reservation at its cut rate, the holder told at acceptance and free to book an increase (POL-07, POL-08). A path whose every leg is a Share link is offered first; a path with a partner leg only when no such path exists. The offer is firm on Share legs: while it is open, their capacity is held for the reservation, at its full rate for the rest of the reservation, as Share's own hold with no funds hold; it counts as committed for new sales, becomes the reservation's on acceptance and is released at once on a decline or lapse. A partner leg is booked only at acceptance, as the buyer is told when the offer is made; if that booking fails, the outcome is as for a declined offer, and any other equivalent path still available is offered (POL-07). |
| **Planned maintenance booking** | A supplier's booking against its own link that takes capacity it will need for planned works, at least `maintenance_notice` (14 calendar days) ahead, on free capacity only: beside the capacity committed and the capacity held for requests in flight (blocked only by the latter, it is rejected with `retry_after`). The supplier may cancel it at any time, freeing the capacity from the first minute boundary at or after the cancellation is recorded (POL-07). It accrues nothing and is excluded from rating and settlement (POL-07). It is not in the committed rate: it never makes a reservation affected and is never a reason to reduce or revoke one; when a permanent reduction takes effect, maintenance that no longer fits beside the reservations within the announced capacity is cut to fit, newest first, and its supplier told (POL-07). Works that need sold capacity are not bookable: the supplier waits for capacity to free or announces a permanent reduction, and works carried out anyway are recorded as an unplanned outage, attributed to the supplier for SLA purposes (POL-07). |
| **Known-at history** | The record that answers "what was known to be reserved on this link at time T", for any past T (POL-19). |
| **Available minute** | A minute in which a capability was probed as required (at least 12 probes, from at least two locations) and at least 90% of its probes succeed, and, for admission and the wallet, in which no account slot had a **failing slot-minute** (at least one probe of the slot in the minute, every one of them failed, however few, a probe failing for this rule only if it got no correct answer within `slot_probe_limit`, 1 s; a slot probed short, none at all included, is a **probing fault**, alerted to network operations, and does not by itself make the minute unavailable): each probe asks for that capability's operation and gets a correct answer within its per-probe limit (POL-06; X2-N1). Probes act as designated Share probe accounts, spread so that every **account slot** of admission and the wallet is probed at least 3 times every minute from at least two locations, on designated probe links of a designated Share probe supplier and on designated probe exporters, excluded from rating, settlement, supplier reports and quarantine reports (POL-06). Each probe account is Share-internal, with a `credit_limit` set by finance; it takes real credit holds and is never invoiced (POL-06). |
| **Serving region** | A region whose edge answers buyers, suppliers, exporters or payment providers. Every availability, latency and recovery target is per serving region; a region that only keeps copies of state and answers none of them has no targets of its own (POL-06). The topology assumed today is in X2's constraints: R1 (Nairobi) and R2 (Mombasa) serve; R3 (GCP Johannesburg) only votes and keeps copies. Its go-live condition is physically diverse fibre: two paths between R1 and R2, one path from R1 to R3 not via R2, and one from R2 to R3 not via R1, all four pairwise without shared risk. |
| **Account slot** | The unit in which admission and the wallet own, serve and fail over accounts, as the platform design defines it (an account belongs to exactly one). Every account slot is probed by at least one designated probe account, at least 3 times every minute from at least two locations; a minute in which a slot got at least one probe and every probe it got failed is not available for admission or the wallet; a slot probed short in a minute is a probing fault, alerted, not by itself unavailability (POL-06). |
| **Declared region disaster** | The loss of a whole region, declared a disaster by network operations with the evidence, approved by a second network operations operator; its end is declared the same way (POL-06, POL-32). For every measurement it lasts from the loss as the system recorded it until the declared end, when the lost region is restored or replaced, or from the start of an unbroken run of at least `disaster_probe_minutes` **measured failing minutes** leading up to the recorded loss, if earlier; a loss never declared is not a disaster. With no recorded loss it may be declared on probe evidence, `disaster_probe_minutes` consecutive measured failing minutes of admission in the region, and then lasts from the start of the first of them. A measured failing minute is one with at least 12 admission probes from at least two locations, fewer than 90% of them succeeding; a minute probed less is unavailable but is not evidence (POL-06). During it, availability and latency are not measured, its minutes are left out of the 99.99% charge-timeliness measurement, each capability recovers within its regional RTO, and RPO 0 still applies: a fact is acknowledged only once durable in two regions, and none acknowledged is lost (POL-06). |
| **Audit record** | The record of a change to money, capacity, a link or an agent key, of a grant or removal of an operator role or an administrator right, of a consent, of a price, weight, partner term or other policy setting, of a webhook endpoint, or of an operator action or read: the principal (by its pseudonymous identifier, never personal data), the time and, for an operator, the role and reason. No principal can alter or remove it through the system, not even by an erasure of personal data; an alteration made below the system, with administrative or physical access to its storage, is detected and recorded, and no reader accepts the altered record. It is kept for the retention period (POL-19, POL-06). Reading the audit trail is a compliance read (POL-32). |

## Pricing, metering and charges

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Product** | What is sold on a route. A **guaranteed product** is priced per reservation; a **best-effort product** (IP transit, CDN traffic) is priced per GB. A guaranteed product may **permit burst**. |
| **Mbps-minute** | One Mbps reserved for one minute. The unit of reservation pricing (POL-01). |
| **Reservation price** | `reservation_price` (POL-01): the price per Mbps-minute of a guaranteed product on a route. A reservation keeps the price it was booked at (POL-27); an added part keeps the price in force when it was added (POL-08). |
| **Usage price** | `usage_price` (POL-01, POL-17): the price per GB of traffic on a best-effort product. Every guaranteed product also carries one, used for permitted burst, for attributable traffic outside a reservation, and for what Share pays a supplier for carried excess. Every usage price changes only with `price_notice` (POL-27). |
| **Accrual** | A reservation's charge for one minute: the sum over its parts of reserved rate × that part's price, whether or not traffic crosses it. |
| **Traffic** | The bytes actually carried. On a route, the egress (delivered) count is the one billed; where the last leg is a partner link Share does not meter, the count at the hand-off to that partner (POL-17). *Replaces "usage" when used of bytes.* |
| **Usage-priced traffic** | Traffic billed per GB: on best-effort products, burst, and traffic attributable to an account outside an active reservation (POL-17). |
| **Burst** | On a product that permits it, the bytes in a minute beyond reserved rate × 60 s, carried best effort on otherwise idle capacity (POL-17). |
| **Excess** | The same measure on a product that does not permit burst: policed at the edge and never billed to the buyer (POL-17). |
| **`usage_rate_cap`** | A per-account, per-product rate policed at the edge that bounds all of the account's usage-priced traffic on the product: burst and traffic with no reserved rate alike; 100 Mbps by default (POL-14). |
| **Flow record** | A report from the network of the bytes one flow carried between a start time and an end time. Its identity is unique for the retention period. Its **measured fields** are its bytes, packets and start and end times; its **attribution keys** are the fields by which its traffic is attributed to an account or a reservation. A superseding record may change only measured fields, its times staying within the minutes the original covered (POL-23). |
| **Record template** | The fields Share's metering accepts in a flow record, which exclude personal data. A received payload that does not conform to it is rejected: the logs and the record kept of it always hold its identity, its keyed digest and the reason, and nothing more of it; the payload itself, if kept, is kept only in the erasable personal-data store (it is not required to be: beyond the caps on payloads kept, or when the store does not take it in time, it is dropped and counted), and is gone from every copy, backups and escrow included, by `dispute_window` + 7 days after its receipt, or earlier once an erasure request covering it is executed (POL-19). |
| **Exporter** | The network device that produces flow records. Each record is identified by its exporter and a sequence number. |
| **Lateness** | How long after the end of the minute it belongs to a flow record arrives; for a record spread over several minutes, after the end of the last minute it was active in. `max_lateness` (POL-14) is the lateness the exposure bound assumes. |
| **Quarantine** | Where traffic that cannot be attributed to an account is kept, unbilled. If attributed within `dispute_window` it is billed at the price of its minute and its supplier is paid; after that, neither. A mistaken attribution may be reversed by finance within `dispute_window`, at Share's cost, the supplier keeping what it was paid; the traffic is not attributed again (POL-17). Every `dispute_window` runs from the end of the traffic's or charge's minute (POL-17). A superseding record of a quarantined record is itself quarantined (POL-23). |
| **Attribution lag** | The time from a record's acceptance by usage ingestion to its being counted in its account's unposted usage, and so in headroom. Bounded by `attribution_lag_max` (POL-14), a timeliness target of POL-06; a record counted later is reported as a breach of the usage exposure bound. |
| **Rating** | Turning accruals and traffic into charges. |
| **Charge line** | The unit a charge is computed, rounded and posted for. Three kinds: one reservation for one minute; for usage-priced traffic, one account and product on one route for one minute (POL-11); and one cancellation fee for one reservation (POL-11). |
| **Charge** | An amount owed by a buyer account for one charge line. Computed exactly and rounded once, down to the micro (POL-11); a cancellation fee is its exact shown amount, with no rounding. |
| **Cancellation fee** | A fee a partner leg imposes on early cancellation, including a partner's minimum term charged that way. Charged only if shown at booking: a plain cancellation fee only on a cancellation; a minimum-term fee on a cancellation inside the term, on a shorten whose new end falls inside it, or when the window the buyer booked ends inside it (the offer showing that fee before booking); who pays depends on who ended the reservation (POL-08): the buyer when it cancels or shortens into the term, when its booked window ends inside the term, or when its reservation is released or terminated; Share when it revokes the reservation, when it cut the holder's rate under POL-07 and the holder then cancels (POL-08), or when the fee was not shown. It is a charge line of its own, posted once by its source reference, not split, and goes wholly to the partner (POL-08, POL-11). |
| **Posted** | Of a charge, amendment or other amount: written to the ledger as a money movement. |
| **Amendment** | A separate, visible change to a charge that has already been posted, with its reason: the line's exact new total rounded down once, less what was already charged for that line (POL-11). Late traffic only ever increases a charge; a price correction can move it either way; a **late POL-07 cut** (minutes of a reservation charged at the uncut rate before a permanent reduction's cut was applied to them) lowers it to the cut rate, every party lowered to its split of the corrected total, as an earnings reduction, not a correction (POL-07, POL-23). |
| **Price correction** | An authorised change, within `dispute_window`, to a price that was set wrongly, with its cause (Share, a supplier, other) and reason recorded, applied to the minutes it affected through amendments. A price locked at booking can only be corrected downwards (POL-23). |
| **Usage exposure** | Usage-priced traffic in records accepted by usage ingestion but not yet posted, at each minute's price (**unposted usage**, POL-18), plus the **lateness bound**: `usage_rate_cap` × (`max_lateness` + `flow_active_timeout` + 60 s of minute granularity + `attribution_lag_max` + admission's 60 s recovery time + the 60 s suspension-to-enforcement target) × the product's highest `usage_price` on any route in force over the bound's span (the window less the enforcement target before now to the enforcement target after), one per account and product (POL-14); 430 s at the defaults. It is deducted from headroom for every account and itemised in its two parts. *Replaces "exposure".* |

## Money

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Micros** | Integer millionths (10⁻⁶) of a currency's major unit, always tagged with the currency (POL-11). |
| **Wallet** | A buyer account's prepaid money, held through the payment partner. |
| **Top-up** | A payment into a wallet. A top-up in another currency is converted at the rate shown, rounded up to the micro; the shown rate is valid for `fx_quote_validity`, after which the partner's rate at notification applies (POL-20). |
| **Reversal** | The payment provider taking back a top-up (a chargeback) (POL-21). A reversal whose payment never arrives within 72 h is escalated, never applied. |
| **Write-off** | Ending a negative balance at Share's cost, automatically, 90 days after the reversal (POL-21), after the invoice's due date (POL-15), or, for any other negative balance, after the balance became negative (POL-21), the clock running only on the **uncovered negative balance** N = max(0, −balance − invoiced and unpaid − charges posted since the last invoice was issued), from when N becomes greater than zero until it returns to zero (POL-21). The loss is booked to Share's own account, but the written-off amount stays recorded as owed: the account stays suspended until a top-up repays it, the repayment going to Share's own account and coming before any overdue invoice (POL-15), and after an invoice write-off the account also loses its credit limit and books prepaid until a finance operator sets one again (POL-21, POL-32). |
| **Ledger** | The record of every money movement. |
| **Money movement** | A set of entries, all in one currency, that moves money between accounts and sums to zero. Each refers to the fact that caused it. *Replaces "journal".* |
| **Ledger balance** | The sum of an account's ledger entries. |
| **Headroom** | Available headroom (POL-18): ledger balance minus funds held (credit holds included) minus usage exposure, plus any credit limit. The headline figure a buyer sees, and the one rule that governs admission and suspension for every account. Charges accrued but not yet posted stay within funds held and are itemised for information only. |
| **`low_balance_warning`** | The headroom below which the buyer is warned: the larger of a quarter of one coverage window's charges and 1 USD (POL-16). |
| **Credit limit** | `credit_limit` (POL-15): an amount a vetted account may owe. Set or removed only by a finance operator, with a reason, and set only on an account that has passed know-your-business checks (POL-32); Share's own probe account counts as having passed them (POL-06). Such an account takes credit holds instead of funds holds and is invoiced monthly, due in `invoice_terms`. |
| **Standing** | Whether an account is **active** or **suspended**. |
| **Suspension** | The standing of an account whose headroom would fall below zero: its hold cannot roll forward (including when a price change needs a larger hold, POL-27), or its usage exposure exceeds what is left; or whose invoice is unpaid after `invoice_terms` (POL-16, POL-15); or that owes a written-off amount (POL-21). Lasts at most `suspension_grace` before its reservations are released. |
| **Resume amount** | The least single top-up after which a suspended account resumes, quoted itemised: any written-off amount owed, each overdue invoice, and what headroom then still needs, with every reservation's full coverage hold restored, to reach `low_balance_warning` (POL-16). A suspension notice, and any rejection while suspended, quotes it. An account suspended for an overdue invoice resumes only when every overdue invoice is paid and that rule also holds; headroom alone never resumes it (POL-16). A top-up to such an account pays its overdue invoices first, oldest first (POL-15). An account owing a written-off amount must first repay it (POL-21): a top-up repays that amount first, then the overdue invoices oldest first, then raises the balance (POL-15). |
| **Commission** | Share's share of each charge. |
| **Share's own account** | The ledger account that receives Share's commission, Share's own deposits and each FX difference (POL-20), and from which Share pays every cost it bears (POL-23). Share keeps it funded at the payment partner so that it covers every receivable Share funds (POL-24): a treasury obligation checked by the daily reconciliation, never a gate on any movement owed to a buyer or supplier. |
| **FX difference** | For a converted top-up or payout, the difference between the micros credited or debited and the USD amount the payment partner reports, posted to Share's own account, positive or negative (POL-20, POL-24). |
| **Split** | Dividing a charge among the parties on its route (suppliers and Share's commission) by their **weights**, under the published apportionment rule (Webster) and tie order: suppliers in route leg order, first leg first, and Share's commission after every supplier (POL-12). A supplier with several legs on one route is one party, its legs' weights summed, in the tie position of its first leg (POL-12). A cost Share bears for usage-priced traffic is paid as the split, among the route's parties, of the charge the traffic would have made, with no commission share posted (POL-23). A cancellation fee is not split. |
| **Payout** | Paying a supplier what it is owed. Complete only when the transfer is confirmed (POL-22). A payout already instructed is never instructed again for the same amounts (POL-29); a transfer the provider executes twice is recovered as a "duplicate payout" debt (POL-22). It goes to the supplier's **payout destination** (the bank or mobile-money account its payouts go to), changed only by a supplier administrator not held in an account recovery; a change applies from the next payout run, and every supplier administrator is told (POL-22, POL-32). |
| **Supplier debt** | An amount a supplier owes back, arising only from a supplier-caused error (POL-22, POL-23), from earnings a late POL-07 cut took back after they were paid out (POL-22, POL-23), or from a transfer the provider executed twice (a debt marked **duplicate payout**, never shown as an earnings reduction and not counted in the deviation report, POL-22); Share bears its own errors in full. It is netted against later payouts and invoiced if open more than 90 days. A supplier balance net of its debts can be negative. |
| **Reconciliation** | The daily proof, for day D by 12:00 UTC on D + 1, that funds at the payment partner = Σ buyer balances, signed (a negative balance is a **receivable** Share has funded) + Σ supplier balances, signed (net of supplier debts; a net negative supplier balance is a receivable too) + Share's own balance there − payouts the partner has executed and the ledger has not yet confirmed + payments the partner has received and reported and the ledger has not yet posted (each matched to its partner credit) − payments the ledger has posted and the partner has not yet received (each alerting if not received within `settlement_lag_max`; one reversed before receipt nets to zero with its reversal, the two matched to each other, with no alert), each in-flight term counted per transaction at the partner's USD amount, with funds at the partner taken in USD as the partner reports them (POL-20); that the **safeguarding rule** holds (Share's own balance covers every receivable it funds, so funds at the partner cover every positive buyer and supplier balance, less payouts the partner has executed and the ledger has not yet confirmed, plus payments the partner has received and the ledger has not yet posted, less payments the ledger has posted and the partner has not yet received); and that every provider transaction matches exactly one ledger movement (a top-up, invoice payment, reversal, payout, supplier-debt payment, or movement of Share's own funds, deposit or withdrawal), and the reverse (POL-24). |
| **Statement** | What an account can see of its own money. For a buyer: charges, amendments and their reasons, top-ups with their conversion rates, reversals and write-offs. For a supplier: each share with the charge (a usage-priced share by an opaque reference of its charge line, never by the buyer's account, POL-33), the weights and the rule and tie order that produced it, payouts, supplier debts with their reasons (a duplicate payout shown as such), residues carried, amounts Share paid it and why, and the deviation from exact proportions. |
