# Glossary

The terms used in the problem statements (`01-discovery/`), the specs (`02-specs/`) and
the [policy register](policy-register.md). Every requirement uses a term only in the sense
given here. Where an older document used a different word, the word it replaces is shown
as *replaces …*.

## Conventions for units and time

| Quantity | Convention |
|---|---|
| Rate | Mbps (10⁶ bits per second). Gbps is written only in prose, as 1 Gbps = 1,000 Mbps. |
| Volume of traffic | Decimal units: KB = 10³ bytes, GB = 10⁹ bytes, TB = 10¹² bytes, PB = 10¹⁵ bytes. At 100 Mbps, one minute carries 0.75 GB. |
| Money | An amount always carries its currency, for example `12.50 USD`. Exact amounts are given in **micros** where precision matters: `1 USD = 1,000,000 micro-USD`. |
| Time of day | `HH:MM` or `HH:MM:SS` in UTC on one calendar day, unless a scenario says otherwise. |
| Window | Half-open: `[10:00, 11:00)` includes 10:00 and excludes 11:00. |
| Durations | Written with their unit (`30 s`, `24 h`, `12 months`, `14 calendar days`). |

## People and parties

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Account** | The entity that pays or is paid: a buyer account or a supplier account. Reservations, booking requests, the wallet, agent keys and statements belong to an account. *Replaces "customer" where the paying entity is meant.* |
| **Account administrator** | A user of a buyer account with the right to issue, change and revoke the account's agent keys and set their limits. Only an account administrator can do these things; an agent key never can (POL-25). |
| **Buyer** | A person, an enterprise or an AI agent acting for a buyer account. *Replaces "client" and "customer" where the person or agent is meant.* |
| **Agent key** | A credential an account issues to an AI agent or other automated client. It acts only for that account, and carries its own `key_spend_limit` per `key_spend_period`, `key_inflight_limit` and `key_rate_limit` (POL-25). An account has at most `account_max_keys` keys. Revoking a key takes effect for new requests within 60 s; its pending requests then fail with the reason "key revoked". |
| **Key spending** | For one agent key in the current `key_spend_period`, counted cumulatively: the charges accrued in the period by the key's reservations, whether a hold covered them or not and including reservations that have since ended, plus the funds currently held for their future charges. Each minute is counted once (POL-25 round 4). *Replaces the round-3 definition (held funds plus only the charges the holds did not cover).* |
| **Key headroom** | The headroom shown to and enforced for an agent key: the lower of the account's headroom and `key_spend_limit` less key spending (POL-25). It can be negative, when the key's own reservations keep accruing past its limit; a key headroom below what a booking needs blocks new bookings and additions by that key only, and never ends, suspends or reduces a reservation or affects the account's other keys (POL-25 round 5). |
| **Agent operator** | The account administrator who issues agent keys and sets their limits. |
| **Principal** | Whoever performs an operation, once authenticated: an account user, an agent key, a supplier user, an operator or an internal service. |
| **Operator** | A member of Share's staff acting under exactly one of the roles of POL-32: **support** (reads, and acts on an account only at its documented request), **network operations** (records outages and restorations, terminates for network abuse), **finance** (price corrections, quarantine attribution, credit limits, money escalations) and **compliance** (terminates for fraud or a sanctions or legal order, and discloses a buyer's identity to a supplier under a legal order, each with a second compliance operator's approval). Each operator action belongs to exactly one role. |
| **Supplier** | An infrastructure provider whose links are sold through the marketplace and who is paid a share of the charges for them. Only a link's own supplier may book maintenance on it or change its capacity (POL-07). |
| **Partner** | A supplier whose link's schedule is kept in the partner's own system, not Share's. A link of this kind is a **partner link**; a link whose schedule Share keeps is a **Share link**. |
| **Partner without holds** | A partner that can only book immediately and finally, and cannot set capacity aside for a time first. A partner whose holds last less than `route_deadline_max` plus `commit_margin` is treated as one (POL-26, POL-28). |
| **Payment provider** | The service a buyer pays through (M-Pesa, Stripe, Adyen and similar). It notifies the marketplace of payments by **payment notification**. |
| **Payment partner** | The licensed institution that holds buyer funds for Share (POL-24). |
| **Network operations** | The team and systems that act on the network: police traffic, block it at the edge, provision connections. |

## Capacity and booking

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Link** | A connection with a fixed **capacity** in Mbps that is sold in slices. |
| **Schedule** | Everything that occupies a link's capacity, minute by minute: confirmed reservations and capacity holds. |
| **Schedule owner** | The single authority that decides every booking on a link, single-link or route (POL-05). There is exactly one at a time per link. |
| **Route** | One or more links, in order, booked for the same window at the same reserved rate, as one unit. A booking on a single link is a route of one leg (POL-05). The legs are numbered in route order, first leg first (POL-12). |
| **Leg** | One link of a route. |
| **Reservation** | A confirmed right to carry traffic up to a reserved rate on every leg of a route during a window. A reservation is created, changed and ended as one unit (POL-08). A buyer cannot book different rates for sub-windows (that is a separate reservation); a change of rate applies from the next minute boundary to the end of the window (POL-08), so a reservation's rate can differ before and after a change. |
| **Reservation reference** | The opaque reference, used for no other reservation, by which a supplier sees and provisions a reservation on its link. A supplier sees the reservation's rate, window, endpoints and this reference, never the buyer's account identity unless the buyer consents (by an account administrator, per named supplier, withdrawable for the future) or a legal order requires it (disclosed by compliance with a second compliance operator's approval) (POL-33). |
| **Part of a reservation** | What one admission added: the original booking, or the difference added by one extension or rate increase (POL-08). Each part keeps its own price and its own age; a decrease removes the most recently added part first (POL-08, POL-07). |
| **Reserved rate** | The rate, in Mbps, that a reservation guarantees. *Replaces "usage" when used of reserved capacity.* |
| **Committed rate** | On one link at one minute: the sum of the reserved rates of the confirmed reservations on it. |
| **Peak** | The highest committed rate, or committed rate plus capacity held, at any minute of a window. |
| **Booking request** | A buyer's request to create or change a reservation. It carries an idempotency key, a deadline and a `wait` setting, and ends with exactly one outcome. |
| **Outcome** | What a booking request ends as: **confirmed** (every leg secured; the reservation exists, or the change is made), **rejected** (it does not fit, or funds, limits or standing do not allow it; the rejection is a counter-offer where one can be computed), **invalid** (the request itself is malformed; it is neither confirmed nor rejected), or **failed** (it could not be completed for another reason, such as its deadline passing ("deadline passed while waiting" for a waiter, with a `retry_after`), the buyer withdrawing it ("withdrawn") or its agent key being revoked ("key revoked"); it holds nothing). A request not confirmed by its deadline has failed as of its deadline, whatever the state of Share's booking process, and every later status reports that (POL-26 round 5). Until it has an outcome, a request is **pending**. *Replaces "admitted", "allowed", "refused" (of a booking request), "fails as an invalid window".* |
| **Not accepted** | A submission that does not become a booking request: refused before anything is decided, because it is unauthenticated, over a rate limit, or reuses an idempotency key for a different request or from a different principal (POL-25, POL-29). No request is created, nothing is held, it has no outcome, and it never changes the request that first used the key. A key that was not already in use may be used again. |
| **Capacity hold** | Capacity set aside on a link for a pending booking request. It counts against the link's capacity. A request takes no capacity hold until its funds hold or credit hold is taken (POL-25 round 5). On a Share link it is released the moment the request has an outcome other than confirmed; on every leg it lapses no later than the request's deadline, by itself (POL-26 round 5). **Capacity in flight** is the Mbps in requests not yet decided, counted once per request at its reserved rate, whatever the number of legs, and limited per key and per account by `key_inflight_limit` and `account_inflight_limit`, the latter never below `account_inflight_floor` (POL-25). *Replaces "hold" where it meant capacity.* |
| **Funds hold** | Money in a wallet set aside, not charged, to cover a reservation's charges for the coverage window (POL-15), or a partner cancellation fee if larger (POL-08), but only while that fee could still be charged: after the partner's minimum term the hold is the coverage (POL-15 round 5). At release after suspension, a shown fee is charged against what is left of the hold, and any shortfall becomes a negative balance. It reduces headroom, not the ledger balance, and it is not a money movement. It is taken before any capacity hold, and released at the request's outcome if the request is not confirmed (POL-25 round 5). For a reservation that starts in the future it is taken at admission and kept until the reservation starts. It is resized when an open-ended reservation's price changes; the price takes effect even if the larger hold cannot be covered (POL-27 round 5). |
| **Credit hold** | For a credit-limit account, the same amount a funds hold would be, taken at admission against the credit limit. It counts as funds held in the headroom formula (POL-15, POL-18). |
| **Coverage window** | The period of future charges that a funds hold covers: `coverage_window` (POL-15), or the whole reservation if shorter. The hold rolls forward as charges accrue, except while the account is suspended. |
| **Deadline** | The time by which a booking request has its outcome. Chosen per request, at least `route_deadline_min`, or `route_deadline_min` + `commit_margin` with a partner leg, and at most `route_deadline_max`; `route_deadline_default` if not stated (POL-26). Every outcome exists by the deadline: a request with a partner leg does not begin committing later than deadline − `commit_margin` (POL-26 round 4). A request still pending at its deadline raises an operational alert. |
| **Waiting** | A request that fits only if a request in flight ends without confirmation waits, pending, within its own deadline (POL-05). So does a request refused definitively by a partner while another Share request holds overlapping capacity at that partner; it asks the partner again when that hold resolves (POL-02 round 5). The priority rule orders requests that are waiting (POL-02). A buyer can ask not to wait (`wait = false`) and is then rejected at once with `retry_after` and the counter-offer computed beside the capacity in flight. |
| **Crossing** | A younger request that holds capacity an older request is waiting for, and is itself waiting. It releases that capacity and waits within its own deadline, keeping its place among waiters (POL-02 round 5). Two requests each holding what the other needs is the pairwise case; the same rule breaks cycles of three or more. A holder that is not waiting is never pre-empted. |
| **Contention guarantee** | Contention alone never makes every competitor lose. It covers requests that wait (the default) and whose deadline outlasts the outcome of the request ahead of them, on Share links and on partner links where Share's own holds cause the contention. A request that asks not to wait, or chooses a deadline shorter than the outcome it waits on, has excluded itself; it is told why ("deadline passed while waiting", with a `retry_after`) or given the counter-offer. A loss caused by a failure (a partner's refusal not caused by another Share request's hold, a fault, an expired deadline after a fault) is not a contention loss; for it, nothing is left held and the buyer is told why (POL-02). |
| **Withdrawal** | A buyer ending its own pending request. The request fails with the reason "withdrawn" and releases what it holds (POL-08). |
| **`ack_budget`** | The time within which every booking request receives a response, final or pending (POL-04). |
| **`retry_after`** | In the immediate rejection of a request that asked not to wait (`wait = false`) and needs capacity held for another request in flight: that request's remaining deadline, after which the capacity is either confirmed or released (POL-05). Also, in a submission not accepted over a rate limit, when the principal may send again (POL-25). |
| **Counter-offer** | The content of a rejection that tells the buyer what they can get instead (POL-10). For a route: the largest rate that fits every leg for the requested window, the earliest window of the requested length, starting at or after the requested start and within the selling horizon, that fits the requested rate on every leg (POL-10 round 5), and the link that blocked it. A `wait = false` rejection computes it beside the capacity in flight. For insufficient funds it states two amounts: the minimum payment to be confirmed (the hold less the headroom), and that minimum plus `low_balance_warning` (POL-10 round 5). |
| **Idempotency key** | A key the buyer chooses for a booking request. It is scoped to the account and identifies its request for `idempotency_retention` (POL-29). The same key from the same principal with the same route, window, rate, product and `wait` setting is the same request; any other reuse within the retention is not accepted. |
| **Source reference** | The reference by which a money-moving input (a payment notification, a transfer confirmation, a payout instruction, an operator correction) is identified, and by which it is idempotent (POL-29). |
| **Window** | The period a reservation covers, `[start, end)`, at minute resolution, or `[start, open)` for an **open-ended** reservation that lasts until cancelled. |
| **Minute boundary** | The start of a whole minute (`HH:MM:00`). |
| **Selling horizon** | How far ahead the marketplace sells: a rolling `selling_horizon` from now (POL-09). *Replaces "horizon" and "booking horizon".* |
| **Affected reservation** | On an unplanned outage, a confirmed reservation on the link at each minute, from the outage's record until its restoration, at which the committed rate exceeds the reduced capacity: every reservation at those minutes alike, none chosen first. An outage that leaves capacity at or above what is committed affects none. Holders active at the record are told within `affected_notice_time`; the holder of a reservation that starts during the outage, within `affected_notice_time` of its start (POL-07 round 5). On a permanent reduction, only the reservations (or parts) that would otherwise not fit the reduced capacity: just enough Mbps, newest part first (POL-07). |
| **Revocation** | Ending a confirmed reservation by Share's decision under POL-07 (permanent reduction, when no equivalent path is accepted, at the end of `revocation_notice`). |
| **Release** | Ending a suspended account's reservations at the end of `suspension_grace`: a termination for non-payment (POL-16). |
| **Termination** | Ending a reservation by Share's decision for a reason in POL-30's closed list: fraud, a sanctions or legal order, or abuse of the network. It records the reason and the operator; charges stop at termination and charges already accrued are not refunded. |
| **Equivalent path** | On a permanent reduction, the path offered to an affected buyer for the whole reservation at its full rate, even when only part of it is affected: the same endpoints and rate, latency and SLA class no worse, at the lower of the original price and the path's price. Share pays the difference only when the path is dearer; the path's parties are split on what is charged. If accepted, all of the reservation moves when the notice ends, or earlier from a minute boundary the holder chooses; if declined or lapsed, only the affected Mbps are removed. The offer lapses after `offer_window`; no answer counts as declined (POL-07 rounds 3 to 5). |
| **Planned maintenance booking** | A supplier's booking against its own link that takes capacity it will need for planned works, at least `maintenance_notice` (14 calendar days) ahead, on free capacity only. It accrues nothing and is excluded from rating and settlement (POL-07). Works that need sold capacity are not bookable: the supplier waits for capacity to free or announces a permanent reduction, and works carried out anyway are recorded as an unplanned outage, attributed to the supplier for SLA purposes (POL-07 round 5). |
| **Known-at history** | The record that answers "what was known to be reserved on this link at time T", for any past T (POL-19). |
| **Available minute** | A minute in which at least 90% of a capability's probes succeed: each probe asks for that capability's operation and gets a correct answer within its per-probe limit (POL-06 rounds 4 and 5; X2-N1). |
| **Audit record** | The record of a change to money, capacity, a link or an agent key, or of an operator action: the principal, the time and, for an operator, the role and reason. It cannot be altered or removed and is kept for the retention period (POL-19, POL-06). |

## Pricing, metering and charges

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Product** | What is sold on a route. A **guaranteed product** is priced per reservation; a **best-effort product** (IP transit, CDN traffic) is priced per GB. A guaranteed product may **permit burst**. |
| **Mbps-minute** | One Mbps reserved for one minute. The unit of reservation pricing (POL-01). |
| **Reservation price** | `reservation_price` (POL-01): the price per Mbps-minute of a guaranteed product on a route. A reservation keeps the price it was booked at (POL-27); an added part keeps the price in force when it was added (POL-08). |
| **Usage price** | `usage_price` (POL-01, POL-17): the price per GB of traffic on a best-effort product. Every guaranteed product also carries one, used for permitted burst, for attributable traffic outside a reservation, and for what Share pays a supplier for carried excess. |
| **Accrual** | A reservation's charge for one minute: the sum over its parts of reserved rate × that part's price, whether or not traffic crosses it. |
| **Traffic** | The bytes actually carried. On a route, the egress (delivered) count is the one billed (POL-17). *Replaces "usage" when used of bytes.* |
| **Usage-priced traffic** | Traffic billed per GB: on best-effort products, burst, and traffic attributable to an account outside an active reservation (POL-17). |
| **Burst** | On a product that permits it, the bytes in a minute beyond reserved rate × 60 s, carried best effort on otherwise idle capacity (POL-17). |
| **Excess** | The same measure on a product that does not permit burst: policed at the edge and never billed to the buyer (POL-17). |
| **`usage_rate_cap`** | A per-account, per-product rate policed at the edge that bounds all of the account's usage-priced traffic on the product: burst and traffic with no reserved rate alike; 100 Mbps by default (POL-14 rounds 3 and 4). |
| **Flow record** | A report from the network of the bytes one flow carried between a start time and an end time. Its identity is unique for the retention period. |
| **Exporter** | The network device that produces flow records. Each record is identified by its exporter and a sequence number. |
| **Lateness** | How long after the end of the minute it belongs to a flow record arrives. `max_lateness` (POL-14) is the lateness the exposure bound assumes. |
| **Quarantine** | Where traffic that cannot be attributed to an account is kept, unbilled. If attributed within `dispute_window` it is billed at the price of its minute and its supplier is paid; after that, neither (POL-17). |
| **Rating** | Turning accruals and traffic into charges. |
| **Charge line** | The unit a charge is computed, rounded and posted for. Three kinds: one reservation for one minute; for usage-priced traffic, one account and product on one route for one minute (POL-11); and one cancellation fee for one reservation (POL-11 round 5). |
| **Charge** | An amount owed by a buyer account for one charge line. Computed exactly and rounded once, down to the micro (POL-11); a cancellation fee is its exact shown amount, with no rounding. |
| **Cancellation fee** | A fee a partner leg imposes on early cancellation, including a partner's minimum term charged that way. Charged only if shown at booking; who pays depends on who ended the reservation (POL-08): the buyer when it cancels early or its reservation is released or terminated; Share when it revokes the reservation, when it cut the holder's rate under POL-07 and the holder then cancels (POL-08 round 5), or when the fee was not shown. It is a charge line of its own, posted once by its source reference, not split, and goes wholly to the partner (POL-08 round 4, POL-11 round 5). |
| **Posted** | Of a charge, amendment or other amount: written to the ledger as a money movement. |
| **Amendment** | A separate, visible change to a charge that has already been posted, with its reason: the line's exact new total rounded down once, less what was already charged for that line (POL-11). Late traffic only ever increases a charge; a price correction can move it either way. |
| **Price correction** | An authorised change, within `dispute_window`, to a price that was set wrongly, with its cause (Share, a supplier, other) and reason recorded, applied to the minutes it affected through amendments. A price locked at booking can only be corrected downwards (POL-23). |
| **Usage exposure** | Usage-priced traffic in records received but not yet posted, at each minute's price (**unposted usage**, POL-18 round 4), plus the **lateness bound**: `usage_rate_cap` × (`max_lateness` + the 60 s suspension-to-enforcement target) × the product's highest `usage_price` on any route, one per account and product (POL-14 rounds 3 and 4). It is deducted from headroom for every account and itemised in its two parts. *Replaces "exposure".* |

## Money

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Micros** | Integer millionths (10⁻⁶) of a currency's major unit, always tagged with the currency (POL-11). |
| **Wallet** | A buyer account's prepaid money, held through the payment partner. |
| **Top-up** | A payment into a wallet. A top-up in another currency is converted at the rate shown, rounded up to the micro; the shown rate is valid for `fx_quote_validity`, after which the partner's rate at notification applies (POL-20). |
| **Reversal** | The payment provider taking back a top-up (a chargeback) (POL-21). A reversal whose payment never arrives within 72 h is escalated, never applied. |
| **Write-off** | Ending a negative balance at Share's cost, automatically, 90 days after the reversal (POL-21) or after the invoice's due date (POL-15). The loss is booked to Share's own account, but the written-off amount stays recorded as owed: the account stays suspended until a top-up repays it, the repayment going to Share's own account, and after an invoice write-off the account also loses its credit limit and books prepaid until a finance operator sets one again (POL-21 round 5, POL-32). |
| **Ledger** | The record of every money movement. |
| **Money movement** | A set of entries, all in one currency, that moves money between accounts and sums to zero. Each refers to the fact that caused it. *Replaces "journal".* |
| **Ledger balance** | The sum of an account's ledger entries. |
| **Headroom** | Available headroom (POL-18): ledger balance minus funds held (credit holds included) minus usage exposure, plus any credit limit. The headline figure a buyer sees, and the one rule that governs admission and suspension for every account. Charges accrued but not yet posted stay within funds held and are itemised for information only. |
| **`low_balance_warning`** | The headroom below which the buyer is warned: the larger of a quarter of one coverage window's charges and 1 USD (POL-16). |
| **Credit limit** | `credit_limit` (POL-15): an amount a vetted account may owe. Set or removed only by a finance operator, with a reason, and set only on an account that has passed know-your-business checks (POL-32). Such an account takes credit holds instead of funds holds and is invoiced monthly, due in `invoice_terms`. |
| **Standing** | Whether an account is **active** or **suspended**. |
| **Suspension** | The standing of an account whose headroom would fall below zero: its hold cannot roll forward (including when a price change needs a larger hold, POL-27 round 5), or its usage exposure exceeds what is left; or whose invoice is unpaid after `invoice_terms` (POL-16, POL-15); or that owes a written-off amount (POL-21 round 5). Lasts at most `suspension_grace` before its reservations are released. |
| **Resume amount** | The top-up after which a suspended account's headroom is at least `low_balance_warning` with every reservation's full coverage hold restored (POL-16 round 4). A suspension notice quotes it. An account suspended for an overdue invoice resumes only when every overdue invoice is paid and that rule also holds; headroom alone never resumes it (POL-16 round 5). An account owing a written-off amount must first repay it (POL-21 round 5). *Replaces the round-3 definition (headroom at least zero).* |
| **Commission** | Share's share of each charge. |
| **Share's own account** | The ledger account that receives Share's commission and from which Share pays every cost it bears (POL-23). Share keeps it funded at the payment partner so that it covers every receivable Share funds (POL-24 round 5). |
| **Split** | Dividing a charge among the parties on its route (suppliers and Share's commission) by their **weights**, under the published apportionment rule (Webster) and tie order: suppliers in route leg order, first leg first, and Share's commission after every supplier (POL-12). A supplier with several legs on one route is one party, its legs' weights summed, in the tie position of its first leg (POL-12 round 5). A cost Share bears for usage-priced traffic is paid as the split, among the route's parties, of the charge the traffic would have made, with no commission share posted (POL-23 round 5). A cancellation fee is not split. |
| **Payout** | Paying a supplier what it is owed. Complete only when the transfer is confirmed (POL-22). A payout already instructed is never instructed again for the same amounts (POL-29). |
| **Supplier debt** | An amount a supplier owes back, arising only from a supplier-caused error (POL-22, POL-23), or from a transfer paid twice (a debt marked **duplicate payout**, never shown as an earnings reduction and not counted in the deviation report, POL-22 round 5); Share bears its own errors in full. It is netted against later payouts and invoiced if open more than 90 days. A supplier balance net of its debts can be negative. |
| **Reconciliation** | The daily proof, for day D by 12:00 UTC on D + 1, that funds at the payment partner = Σ buyer balances, signed (a negative balance is a **receivable** Share has funded) + Σ supplier balances, signed (net of supplier debts) + Share's own balance there − payouts the partner has executed and the ledger has not yet confirmed; that the **safeguarding rule** holds (Share's own balance covers every receivable it funds, so funds at the partner cover every positive buyer and supplier balance); and that every provider transaction matches exactly one ledger movement, and the reverse (POL-24 rounds 3 to 5). |
| **Statement** | What an account can see of its own money. For a buyer: charges, amendments and their reasons, top-ups with their conversion rates, reversals and write-offs. For a supplier: each share with the charge, the weights and the rule and tie order that produced it, payouts, supplier debts with their reasons (a duplicate payout shown as such), residues carried, amounts Share paid it and why, and the deviation from exact proportions. |
