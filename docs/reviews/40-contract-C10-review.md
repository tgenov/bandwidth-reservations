## Verdict

**ACCEPT WITH CHANGES.** 0 Blockers, 10 Majors, 16 Minors.

The core of C10 is sound and faithful to RFC-002: one answer within `ack_budget`, idempotency
by (account, key) with the POL-29 fingerprints, the five status-read guarantees, the reporting
gate, uniform `401`/`404`, ending changes never queued, and the reason codes of RFC-004
§A5.1 in their order. The new surface added by the settlements (M2 `quote` and
`TERMS_CHANGED`, M3 offer answers, M8 documented-request idempotency) is where it breaks:
where a quote lives and how it survives an owner change is not said, the idempotency key
namespace is shared ambiguously between five operation families, the "fee shown before
booking" rule of POL-08 is still unmet for a booking that cites no quote, and the errata list
names only RFC-002 although RFC-005's deny-by-default matrix (C2) would refuse `quote` and
every offer answer at the edge as written. A withdrawal that reaches the owner before the
booking it targets answers `404` and the booking then proceeds, which an agent will read as
"nothing was booked".

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Location | Finding | Proposed change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R40-01 | Major | §quote, Idempotency (quote row), Ownership, M2 | **Where a quote lives is not specified, and the choice matters for failover and for `ack_budget`.** Quote idempotency ("the first `Quote`, unchanged, even past `valid_until`", 24 h) and "an unknown `quote_id` … is `404`" at `book` both need the quote to survive the owner that issued it. Not durable: after an owner takeover, slot move or re-home (seconds, routine under RFC-006), a `book` citing a quote from a minute ago gets `404`. That is not an outcome, the key is not consumed, and every retry under the key gets `404` again until the agent re-quotes; CT-C08 does not tell it to. Durable in the account log: one more entry per quote in the account's single lane, whose single-link p99 is already 176/186/196 ms against 200 ms (RFC-002 §8.1, RB-16). A quote-before-book agent (CT-C08 recommends it) doubles the lane's writes for its account. Neither RFC-002 nor RFC-006 sizes it | Decision D3: no log write. `quote_id` is an owner-issued authenticated token: a MAC under a per-account-slot key held by every owner-lease holder of the slot (not the edge), over (account, candidate digest, `shown` digest, `valid_until`, key version). `book` verifies it statelessly: wrong account or bad MAC is `404`, as today. It survives every owner change. Quote idempotency: a replay under the same key returns the first `Quote` while the owner still has it in memory, otherwise a re-issued quote for the same candidate, equal to the first if the terms are unchanged. This narrows M2's "unchanged, even past `valid_until`", so the orchestrator must confirm it. If M2 is kept literally, quotes go in a projection table written outside the account lane, like marker-only transactions, and RFC-002 §14 and RFC-006 size it (est.) |
| R40-02 | Major | Idempotency table; status; withdraw; M2, M3, M8 | **One key namespace or five?** Every row reads "(account, `Idempotency-Key`)". (a) If one namespace is shared: an agent that uses one key per intent for both `quote` and `book` (the natural reading of CT-C01) gets `409 key_reuse_different_request` on its `book`. (b) `GET /v1/requests/{key}` and `withdraw` on a key used for a quote, an offer answer or a documented request have no defined answer: `RequestStatus.request_kind` is only booking or change. (c) Documented requests are deduplicated at the console (RFC-005 §7.5), not by the owner's index, so they cannot share that namespace anyway | Decision D2: one namespace per operation family, (account, family, key), with four families: **booking and change** (the only one `status` and `withdraw` read), **quote**, **offer answer** and **documented request**. State it in the Idempotency section and in CT-C01. A key reused across families is independent. Add CT-P23: the same key used for quote then book gives one quote and one request |
| R40-03 | Major | §book "Shown figures", §change "Fees", M2; POL-08, P3-S74, P2-S50 | **A booking that cites no quote is never shown its fees before booking, yet the buyer pays them.** POL-08: "The offer shown before booking states a fee that the requested window will incur … Share absorbs partner fees it did not show". C10 makes `quote_id` optional. Without it, `shown` reaches the buyer only in the answer, after the decision, and `fees_due` gives payer `buyer` for a window-end or cancellation fee. So the contract either charges the buyer a fee it was not shown before booking (against POL-08 and P3-S74 row 1), or, if "shown" means "in the answer", the sentence "a request is never charged terms that were not shown to it" means nothing. Under the opposite reading Share absorbs every minimum-term fee of every agent that never quotes | Decision D1: a `book`, `extend` or `increase` whose locked terms include **any partner-leg fee** and that cites no `quote_id` is `rejected`, reason `quote_required`, recorded under the key (E1′), nothing held. `shown` carries the current terms and a fresh `quote_id`, so the agent resubmits under a new key citing it, with no extra round trip. Routes without partner fees need no quote. Then "shown" has one meaning: present in a `Quote` the request cites. CT-P21 gains the `quote_required` row |
| R40-04 | Major | `PartnerLegTerms.fee?: ShownFee`; P2-S50, M2 ("every shown fee") | **A leg can carry only one fee.** P2-S50: "a partner leg that imposes a minimum term **and** a cancellation fee … the price shows the minimum term and the cancellation fee". The catalogue's partner terms are (minimum term, cancellation fee, …) (RFC-002 §13.2), and M2 says "every shown fee". `fee?` with a single `kind` cannot show a leg that has both a minimum term (charged as a fee) and a plain cancellation fee after it, so P2-S50 is not satisfiable on the wire | `fees: ShownFee[]` per leg (empty: no fee). State which fee one event makes due: a cancellation inside the term pays the minimum-term fee, after the term the plain fee, and at most one fee per reservation and leg (RFC-002 §9.5). `ChangeEffect.fees_due` already lists by `kind`. CT-P16 gains a leg with both fees |
| R40-05 | Major | Common request headers; 400 row; §withdraw (M5) | **The header table requires `Idempotency-Key` on every `POST` "except offer answers' reads".** Withdraw is a `POST` with no key of its own (M5), and the 400 row makes a missing key `malformed`. A provider implementing the table literally refuses every withdrawal with `400`, so no buyer can stop a pending request. Offer answers, which M3 does key, are exempted instead; offer reads are `GET`s anyway | "On every `POST` except `withdraw` (which names its target's key in the path, M5)". The 400 row says the same. Add CT-P24: withdraw without a header is accepted |
| R40-06 | Major | §withdraw, 404 row; RFC-002 §7.1, §3.1; RFC-005 C1 `context_ttl` | **A withdrawal can overtake its booking, and the booking then proceeds.** The edge forwards a submission once, and a `Moved`, `StaleEpoch` or `Moving` re-route can delay it by seconds; a client retry can also still be in flight. If `withdraw(K)` reaches the owner first, the owner answers `404` "no such request". The booking under K then arrives (anything up to `context_ttl`, 60 s, after the edge built its context) and is decided normally. An agent that withdrew on timeout ("cancel what I asked") gets `404` and believes nothing was booked, and a reservation it did not want is confirmed and charged. `404` on `status` has the same hazard, but status is only a read; `withdraw` is the buyer's only instrument to stop the request, and here it silently has no effect | Decision D4: `withdraw` of a key unknown to the account records a **pre-emptive withdrawal** under (account, booking family, K) with the principal: an E1′ with outcome `failed`, `withdrawn` and no fingerprint. The answer is that `RequestStatus`. A later submission under K by the same principal is answered with it, whatever its body; by another principal, `409 key_reuse_other_principal`. Counted toward `key_rate_limit` like any submission. RFC-002 erratum (AI-40-3). Add CT-P25: withdraw then a delayed book under the same key leads to no reservation |
| R40-07 | Major | §book validation, ReasonCode, endpoint table (`404` on book); RFC-002 §3.2 | **No outcome is defined for a booking that names no sellable route.** Missing cases: unknown `link_id`, legs not forming a catalogue route (prices and POL-12 split weights are per catalogue route, RFC-002 §9.4), `product_id` unknown or not sold on the route (or a per-GB best-effort product), duplicate legs, empty `legs`, and a **non-probe principal naming a probe link** (RFC-005 §3.5 restricts probe keys, but nothing stops a real buyer from booking a probe link that rating then excludes). There is no reason code for any of them. The `404` listed for `book` is defined only for resources inside an account. Implementations will diverge between `404`, `400` and `invalid`, and the probe-link case could let a buyer use a link that is excluded from rating | Decision D6: `invalid`, recorded (E1′), with reasons `unknown_route` (covering unknown link, no catalogue route, duplicate or empty legs, and a probe link for a non-probe principal, which reveals nothing about probe designation) and `product_not_on_route`. Checked first in RFC-002 §3.2. `404` on `book` and `change` only for `quote_id` (and the reservation, for `change`). RFC-002 erratum |
| R40-08 | Major | §documented request "Errors: `not_found` (target outside the tenant or absent)"; RFC-005 §5.1, §7.5, C2 | **The console cannot answer `not_found` without an owner read, and adding one creates a timing oracle.** C2 `decide` is a pure function of (context, operation, resource) and does not know whether a reservation exists. The console keeps no account state, and RFC-005 §7.5 specifies no existence check. To answer `404` the console must ask the owner: a different path, and a different time, from the uniform miss of RFC-005 §5.1 (X1-S02 "the same answer", which RFC-005 makes true by one code path). The check is also unnecessary: the support action is routed to the requester's own partition, where another account's reservation is a miss | Decision D5: the intake checks only the principal (C2) and well-formedness, records any target string, and answers `201`. Add to CT-P20: a documented request naming another account's reservation gets `201`, and the later support action is refused with the same answer as for a non-existent reservation |
| R40-09 | Major | Status line ("RFC-002 takes `quote` and `TERMS_CHANGED` as errata"), Settled M2, M3, M7, M8 | **The errata list is incomplete, and one missing erratum makes the settled operations unreachable.** RFC-005 §4.2 is deny-by-default ("Anything not listed is denied"), and C2 evaluates it at the edge. ## Decisions taken

| # | Decision | Balance |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | A booking or addition whose locked terms include any partner-leg fee must cite a `quote_id`; otherwise `rejected`, `quote_required`, recorded, nothing held, with current `shown` and a fresh `quote_id` (R40-03) | Buyers and agents: one extra submission only on fee-bearing partner routes, and never a surprise fee. Suppliers: unaffected. Share: no absorbed fees from agents that skip the quote. Operability: one rule, testable |
| D2 | Idempotency namespaces per family: booking and change (the only one `status` and `withdraw` read), quote, offer answer, documented request (R40-02) | Agents can use one key per intent without self-collision. No cross-family leaks |
| D3 | `quote_id` is a stateless MAC token under a per-slot owner key; quotes are never written to the account log (R40-01). Narrowing M2's "unchanged even past `valid_until`" to "the first quote while held in memory, else an equal re-issue" needs the orchestrator's confirmation | Protects the account lane's `ack_budget` margin and survives owner changes. Safety still rests on the term comparison at E1 |
| D4 | A withdrawal of an unknown key records a pre-emptive `failed withdrawn` under the key (R40-06) | "Withdraw" means "this key will not book". Cost: one entry per such withdrawal, rate-limited |
| D5 | The documented-request intake makes no existence check; the owner checks when support acts (R40-08) | Keeps X1-S02's uniform miss. Support refuses a bad target later at no cost to anyone |
| D6 | `unknown_route` and `product_not_on_route` are `invalid` outcomes, recorded; a probe link named by a non-probe principal is `unknown_route` (R40-07) | Deterministic answers for agents. Probe links stay outside real sales without revealing them |
| D7 | No documented `withdraw` (R40-21) | It cannot act within a 60 s deadline |
| D8 | Any principal of the account permitted to change the reservation may answer its offer (probe keys only for probe reservations); the other-principal rule applies only to key reuse | Matches the permission matrix's "own account" reading for changes |
