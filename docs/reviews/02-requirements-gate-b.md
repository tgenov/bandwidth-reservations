## Verdict

**FAIL.** The rewrite after round 1 is real. Verification metadata, transport and storage
detail are gone from P3, P4 and X2. Every worked example checks out: accruals, holds,
headroom, low-balance threshold, proportional spreading, rounding down, payout residue,
netting, and both Webster rows plus the tie (checked by sequential Sainte-Laguë). Nearly
every policy in scope has a scenario. The baseline still cannot be authoritative for money.
One definition breaks settlement outright: a usage-priced charge is defined per account per
minute, but the split is per route (GB-01). Several money flows also have no rule, so an
RFC would have to invent business policy. These are: funds for future-dated reservations,
credit-limit accounts and their invoices, partner minimum terms and fees, price
corrections, supplier-caused corrections, FX rounding, quarantined traffic once
attributed, the usage-exposure bound for traffic with no reserved rate, and a grace period
the money no longer covers after a reversal. Money has no idempotency or concurrency
requirements beyond top-ups and payout runs. POL-06's recovery times use up more than the
whole monthly availability budget. The structure is sound and the list below is bounded.
Answer the Policy questions in the register, apply the Fixes, and re-gate only the
affected scenarios.
