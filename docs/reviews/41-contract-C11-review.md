## Verdict

**REJECT.** 3 Blockers, 12 Majors, 14 Minors. Every fix stays inside C11, plus RFC errata. None of them changes a settled decision's outcome.

Parts II and IV are close to right. They give an exactly-once effect by contiguous
per-stream sequences, deterministic outbox rows, watermarks only from resolved markers,
and no payload on any topic. The Blockers are in the wire seam and in the new feed:

- **Record identity is not a function of the retransmitted bytes.** The IPFIX init time
  arrives in an options record, and templates are re-declared per session, so neither
  travels with the message. A resend after an exporter reboot, or after a "no epoch"
  rejection followed by a correction, charges the same traffic twice.
- **NetFlow v9 sequence numbers count packets, not records.** The identity
  `k × 2³² + q + i` collides between consecutive packets, so most v9 records are
  acknowledged as duplicates. They are never charged, and the 64-bit digest check then
  floods quarantine with "identity conflict".
- **`provisioning.domains.v1` has no persistence, no completeness signal and no recorded
  position.** A stage A instance started after the topic's retention treats every domain as
  unassigned. The contract then sends all traffic, bindings or not, to "no binding"
  quarantine. Consumer lag on this topic quarantines valid traffic at once, and decisions
  cannot be reproduced (P3-N9).

| F41-01 | **Blocker** | Part I `BatchItem.exportMessage`; "Templates are session-scoped"; Units row "Flow times"; template table row "System init time"; Versioning ("a verbatim resend years later must map to the same identity") | **Identity and decoding depend on session state that is not in the verbatim message.** IPFIX carries `systemInitTimeMilliseconds` (160) in an **options record**, usually sent once or periodically, not in each data message. Templates are re-declared **as currently in force** on each new session. Three traces fail. (a) **Double charge on reboot.** Message M (epoch E1) commits in R1 and the ack is lost. The router reboots, and the relay gets init time I2. It reconnects to R2, sends its current templates and options (I2), then resends M verbatim. Ingestion decodes M under I2 and opens epoch E2, which gives a new identity: **accepted again, charged twice**. (b) **Double charge by correction.** Same start, but the new session has seen no options record yet. M is rejected "no epoch" and acknowledged. The exporter corrects it, and the correction gets the identity `{kind:"correction", rawRef}`, which cannot be deduplicated against M's: **charged twice** (P3-S14, P3-S16 violated). (c) **Wrong bytes.** After a reboot the template ID is redefined with a different field order, so a resent M is decoded with the wrong template. The result is accepted with wrong bytes (if M never committed) or becomes a false identity conflict | Make each item self-describing: `exportMessage: { message: bytes; context: bytes }`. `context` holds the exact template sets and options records (init time) **in force when the message was first exported**, kept by the exporter with the message and resent byte-identical. Ingestion computes identity and decodes **only** from (context, message). Session templates are a cache, never authoritative for a retransmission. A message whose context lacks an init time is rejected "no epoch". Add exporter conformance test: reboot between send and resend, resend to the other region, all `duplicates`. Add provider test: a template ID redefined on a new session does not change a resent message's decode. RFC-003 §3.1 and §3.2 erratum (AI-41-1) |

## Decisions taken

| ID | Decision | Balance |
|---|---|---|
| D41-1 | A correction of a rejected record is accepted only before the corrected record's last minute end + `dispute_window`. Otherwise it is refused "outside dispute_window" and recorded | Buyers and AI agents get a fixed horizon for charges, the same as supersessions and finance attributions (POL-17, POL-23). Suppliers have 30 days to fix a rejection, ample since every rejection is acknowledged at once with its reason. Share avoids amending closed periods past the attribution log's retention |
| D41-2 | Whatever the domain state, a part with no binding is held, never quarantined at once. At the end of the hold its reason ("domain not of exporter" or "no binding") is taken from the covered state | Keeps S-1's distinct reason and evidence. Removes revenue loss from consumer lag at the cost of at most `binding_grace` (5 min, est.) more delay before a quarantine report. Unattributable traffic belongs to no account, so no headroom is delayed |
| D41-3 | NetFlow v9 stays in `protocolVersion` 1, with packet-level identity (F41-02) | Many suppliers' routers export only v9, and a relay cannot convert to IPFIX without breaking verbatim retransmission. The cost is a per-format identity rule and test |
| D41-4 | An unparseable remainder or whole message is one `RawRef` (`record = −1`). Its correction may carry several records, and the whole correction is deduplicated by the raw reference | The supplier can recover every record in a corrupted message. "Charged once" (P3-S30) holds per correction |
| D41-5 | Credit is a window of unacknowledged batches. Credit returns at ack or after `ingestRetryTimeoutMs`. The edge forgets an unacknowledged batch at that timeout | Exporters can always retransmit, and the edge's memory is bounded. Ingestion's range set already makes resends harmless |
| D41-6 | A bound part with no observation-point → link mapping is attributed (`linkId` null), and provisioning is alerted | The buyer's charge does not depend on the link, and quarantining would cost suppliers revenue for a provisioning omission. Quarantine reports simply lack a link |
