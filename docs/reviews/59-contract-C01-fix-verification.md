### Defects

| V59-1 | Major | §2.5 `ReportMarker`; §3.4 "Marker chain" rules 3–4; §2.4 `EntryInput.pendingAnswered`; CP-7a; §10.3 RFC-002 row on `pendingAnswered` | **The C04 v2 extension is contradicted on the case it was built for.** C04 says the following: after an acquisition **or `adopt`**, `prevReportPosition` is the predecessor's last `report_through`, and the first marker lists **every** covered event above it (the new holder cannot know its predecessor's `pending` answers). C04 types the field `HlcTs \| null` and names the list `pending_answered`. C01 rule 3 instead starts the new chain at the new holder's initial position, and rule 4 lists only requests "the slot answered `pending`". Trace (C04 PO-5 trace 2, P2-S08/S09): E5 for q commits at `t`, the holder sends `pending`, then crashes before the covering marker. The successor's initial position T ≥ t, and its first marker covers (T, P]. No marker's interval holds `t`, and `answered_pending` is false. So no webhook is sent, and the notifier waits forever for a first covering marker. The same happens for X's requests answered `pending` by A and decided in B after `adopt` | Rule 3: "the first marker after an acquisition has `prevReportPosition` = the predecessor's last `report_through` (null if the slot has none), and `pendingAnswered` lists **every** outcome with `ts` in its interval". Add: "B's first marker covering an entry of X after `adopt` lists every outcome of X in its interval". Rule 4 keeps "answered `pending`" only within one holder's epoch. Type `prevReportPosition: HlcTs \| null`. Use one field name in both contracts (`pendingAnswered` in C01, with C04 importing it, or the reverse). | V59-2 | Major | §3.8 last sentence of the `slotOf` paragraph ("`s+n` … `MOVED(s)` otherwise") | **New upper-half accounts cannot be created while `s` is splitting.** `s` answers a creation with no head `MOVED(s+n)` (§3.8 handshake, CP-10). `s+n` answers anything without an adopted head naming it `MOVED(s)`, and a non-existent account has no head. So the router loops between the two slots until `s` is split. This contradicts "every creation in either slot appends with an implicit `rowAbsent(head)`" and the RFC-002 erratum ("creation of upper-half accounts only in `s+n`") | "`s+n` serves an account whose head names it and is adopted, **creates an upper-half account that has no head row (implicit `rowAbsent(head)`)**, answers `MOVING` for one whose head names it unadopted, and `MOVED(s)` otherwise". | V59-5 | Minor | §3.2 "Integrity stops" ("a batch … in flight when `Stop` commits fails its read refresh and restarts … from then, no entry of the partition can commit"); CP-6 ("a batch in flight when `Stop` commits does not commit an entry") | Under CockroachDB, `Stop`'s write to `owner_slot` is pushed above the batch's read timestamp. ### Settled points and earlier fixes

M1 … M13 are unchanged. The M8 refinement (closure topic, no separate republish role) stays
within the decision, as §12 says. No fix in draft 2 breaks another. V59-2 and V59-4 come from
combining R47-05 with R47-02 and R47-04. V59-6 predates draft 1, and the R47-03 fence now
relies on it.

## Verdict

**ACCEPT WITH CHANGES.** Both Blockers hold on re-trace (P1-S78 and A7 across moves; the
`adopted` fence; P2-S10). Seven defects remain. Each is a precise local edit of a few lines
(V59-1 rewrites marker-chain rule 3, adds one sentence and changes one type; V59-6 moves one
column). **The orchestrator may apply them directly, with no further round.** V59-1 must be
applied consistently with C04 v2, which is in re-review 58, so the field name has to be
settled in both contracts.
