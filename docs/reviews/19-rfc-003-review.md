## Verdict

**ACCEPT WITH CHANGES.** 0 Blockers, 7 Majors, 13 Minors (20 findings).

Every worked example in the RFC recomputes correctly. That covers P3-S01 … S73 where
amounts are given, P3-S37's 370 s / 46,250 / 48,250, P4-S110's 8 − 4 = 4, P3-S51's
17,142 / 12,857, P3-S70's boundary and P3-S68's 170 s / 190 s. The pico-USD price unit and
the ELA give exact floors. Exactly-once by source reference, amendment by new total, and
closure on resolved timestamps all hold under the crash points traced (owner change
mid-batch, lost direct publish, takeover between commit and acknowledgement, stalled stage
A partition).

The defects are on the edges where rating's amounts become someone else's money:

1. **Share-borne cost lines**: downward amendments have no posting rule and would claw
   back final supplier earnings (F3-01). A correction of the buyer's price on a moved
   reservation pays the path's suppliers twice (F3-02).
2. **Price corrections**: they replace the result of the move and open-ended rules, so a
   "downward" correction can raise a moved reservation's charge above the path price
   (F3-03).
3. **Exact pairing**: rating's one batch mixes reservation and usage lines. RFC-004 posts
   them in two movements, so `whole` is never true and the in-flight usage is counted twice
   every minute, which RQ3-12 forbids (F3-04).
4. **Fees**: a plain cancellation fee is charged on any shorten (F3-05). A window that ends
   inside a minimum term gets no line at all (F3-06).
5. **Splits**: after a move, no source is defined for the new path's route and weights
   (F3-07).

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Location | Finding | Proposed change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F3-01 | Major | §5.5 last paragraph; §7 charge-line feed; RFC-004 M12, M13 | "Cost lines are amended like charge lines" with no reason, cause or direction rules. A downward amendment of a carried-excess or dearer-path cost line can come from a `usage_price` correction down, a superseding record that lowers B, a correction of the path's price or a reversal. It has no posting rule in RFC-004 (M12/M13 are positive and have no amendment sequence in their source reference). Posted as a normal-mode amendment, it would debit suppliers whose earnings are final (POL-23). RFC-003's cost source reference has an amendment sequence and RFC-004's does not | Specify cost-line amendments: reason and cause as for charge lines. Upward: posted as M12/M13 for the increment. Downward with cause Share or "other": recorded in rating, emitted with amount 0 to post (supplier earnings final; Share has already paid). Downward with cause a supplier (its own exporter's superseding record): that supplier's own reduction as a `fault` debt, as M9. Align the source reference with RFC-004 (add the amendment sequence to M12/M13), and raise it as a joint item |
| F3-02 | Major | §5.5 dearer path; §5.2 rule 4 | The difference is "path charge − the reservation line's charge" and "a price correction changes both". When the buyer's price is corrected down (M8/M9), the buyer's line falls, so the difference rises and Share pays the path's suppliers again for value they already keep. In the trace, suppliers receive 400 for a path charging 300. POL-23's literal text allows this reading, so it is also a requirements issue (RQ-19-3) | Compute the difference against the buyer's charge under rules 1 to 3 (before any price correction of the buyer's price). A correction of the buyer's price amends only the buyer's line. A correction of the path's price version amends only the difference (with F3-01's rules) |
| F3-03 | Major | §5.2 rule 4; §6.3 "Locked prices only downwards"; AI3-7 read model | A correction "replaces the result of 1 to 3", but it is validated only against the part's **locked** price. (a) After a move, a correction below the lock but above the path price (1.8 on lock 2, path 1.5) raises the charge, against POL-07 step 4 and POL-23. (b) After an open-ended re-price (2 → 3), a correction to 2.5 lowers the effective price but is refused as "above the lock". The read model of locked prices has neither the path price nor `oe_effective` | Validate and apply against the **effective** price per covered minute (the result of rules 1 to 3). A reservation correction is accepted only if it is at or below that price at every covered minute, and applies as `min(corrected, effective)`. The catalogue's read model must then carry moves (path price, effective minute) and open-ended re-prices, or the check moves to rating, which has the model |
| F3-04 | Major | §5.8 (one batch per tick with reservation lines, usage lines, amendments and fees); §5.9 in-flight reduction; RFC-004 §B3.1 | RFC-004 posts the batch's reservation lines as one M4 per (account, m) and the rest as a separate mixed movement. It also splits any batch above 5,000 lines (always the case for an R6 account), each part `whole = false`. So the movement never posts b "whole", and Δ_b is never applied. Exposure is overstated by the in-flight usage every minute, which RQ3-12 forbids and §5.9's exactness proof assumes away. Publish-before-emit also gates reservation-only batches, which carry nothing into U | Split the stream. **Reservation batches**: reservation lines of minute m, not gated by publish-before-emit, posted as M4. **Usage batches**: usage lines, amendments, fees and cost lines, gated, one in flight, kept at or below the movement limit and posted whole. Define `whole` and Δ_b over usage batches only. Raise jointly with RFC-004 (AI-19-1). Drop the 1,700/s of figures that reservation-only accounts would otherwise need |
| F3-05 | Major | §5.4 term definition and table ("Cancelled or shortened by the account") | A plain cancellation fee's term "until the window ends" always applies at a shorten's new end, so every shorten of such a reservation charges the fee. P3-N12 and POL-08 name only "a shorten whose new end falls inside a partner leg's minimum term", and P3-N12 says "none charged otherwise". POL-08's "as a cancellation of the remaining part" makes it ambiguous (RQ-19-1) | Charge a plain cancellation fee only on a cancellation (including a shorten to or before the next boundary). Charge a minimum-term fee on a cancellation or a shorten into the term. Add a row to the §9 fee test |
| F3-06 | Major | §5.4 end causes; §7 lifecycle feed | "Ended at its window's end" while a shown minimum term still runs (a 10-day window on a 30-day-term leg) gives no line. P3-N12 does not let the buyer be charged, yet the partner bills Share, so a real cost Share bears is never posted (POL-23 "every cost Share bears is paid ... as its own ledger movement") | Meanwhile emit a Share-borne fee line (M11, reason "window ended inside minimum term"). Raise RQ-19-2 (buyer pays, or RFC-002 refuses such windows) |
| F3-07 | Major | §5.2 rule 3, §5.5, §7 consumed lifecycle feed ("moves ... with the path's shown price and effective minute") | P3-S59 requires the moved reservation's lines to be split among the new path's parties, and the difference among them too. RFC-003 never says which route ID and weights version its reservation lines carry after a move. The consumed contract has no route for the path, and a routing path is not shown to be a catalogue route with weights. Settlement would split by the old route, paying the exiting supplier | The consumed lifecycle feed's move event carries the path's catalogue route ID (RFC-002 already sends "moved (path, price)"). From the move's effective minute, rating labels reservation lines and dearer-path cost lines with it and its weights version. A path must be registered as a catalogue route with weights before it is offered (AI-19-2) |
| F3-08 | Minor | §5.9 lateness bound; §7 unposted-usage feed; RFC-004 §A3, §B12 | Two owners compute LB. RFC-003 takes the highest price "from now to the end of that window" (looking forward). RFC-004 takes the price in force at F3 (current), and requires rating's displayed value to equal its own. Both miss the past: the window is mostly traffic already carried (up to about 310 s back), so for about 310 s after a notified **decrease**, uncounted traffic at the old price exceeds LB | Define LB once (RFC-004's F3) with the highest price over `[now − (window − 60 s), now + 60 s]`, and have rating stop publishing LB, or publish it by the same function. Raise RQ-19-5 |
| F3-09 | Minor | §5.9 "per account and product in the account's attachment set" | U(X) is published only for products with a live binding. A finance attribution to a product the account does not attach, or lines left after a binding ends, are charged but never counted in U. Headroom is then overstated until they are posted | Publish U for every product with a line not fully posted, as well as the attachment set |
| F3-10 | Minor | §5.9 attribution lag; X2-S22 measure | Finance-attributed parts (accepted days earlier) and quarantine supersessions are reported as attribution-lag breaches, and they retroactively fail the X2-S22 minute measure. The breach feed then floods finance and risk with false breaches | Exclude parts that reach rating through a finance attribution from attribution-lag breaches and from the measure (at acceptance they belonged to no account). | F3-11 | Minor | §5.2 active minutes; §5.6; §5.7 trigger table | A release at the end of `suspension_grace`, or a revocation at the notice end, is effective at a time fixed in advance. If the account owner commits it late (failover), RFC-000 §4.5 makes its effective minute no earlier than its commit, so the buyer pays minutes past the grace. No amendment trigger exists for "end known late" | State that the effective end is `max(nominal, next boundary after commit)` and that the extra minutes stand (consistent with P3-S09 "after its release"), or add a downward amendment reason "end recorded late", cause Share. Also state that rating ends accrual at E11's effective minute without waiting for RFC-002's E12 gate ("published as effective only once E12 is committed") |
| F3-12 | Minor | Benefits (Timeliness), §5.12 table, R3-9 | "Reservation lines posted about 6 s after their minute", and "closure no longer waiting on an account owner's failover". But emission waits for the owner (publish-before-emit), and RFC-004 §B3.1 budgets about 1 min, assuming rating emits within about 30 s. The claims disagree | Fixed by F3-04 (reservation batches not gated). Align the posting estimate with RFC-004 |
| F3-13 | Minor | §5.10 cold re-rate | "Emits `C − posted` as the next amendment". Without the log, rating knows neither the next amendment sequence (a collision is excluded by the ledger as a conflict) nor the per-cause decomposition (M8/M9 own mode, supplier debts). | F3-14 | Minor | §5.7 "emitted total E"; §5.8 excluded lines | An excluded line (a content conflict) stays in E, so every later amendment `C − E` leaves the ledger short of C for good | On an exclusion, re-base E for that line on the acknowledged posted total and alert. | F3-15 | Minor | §5.2 rules 2 and 3 | After a move, it is undefined which price series drives open-ended re-pricing ("its price" in `min(its price, path price)`), and whether the path price ever changes. | F3-16 | Minor | §1.2, §3.6 ingestion sizing | "At most about 50 transactions/s per slot" with one in flight and a 30 ms commit gives about 33/s. Not marked est. as a derived figure | Recompute: per slot, 1 / (commit time) transactions/s. Correct the batch sizes and the 26,000/s total |
| F3-17 | Minor | §5.11 memory | "About 64 B" per closed ELA is unmarked and low. | F3-18 | Minor | §2 "verified interval arithmetic" | Over-engineering for Node.js: there is no native 128-bit integer, so 64.64 fixed point is itself `BigInt` arithmetic, and the exact rational floor with `BigInt` costs about the same. | F3-19 | Minor | §5.11, R6; RFC-004 §B10 | One batch in flight per account, plus RFC-004's 5,000-line movement limit: an R6 account (1 M lines a minute) needs about 200 sequential postings a minute. That is unverified against the 60 s tick and X2-S13 | With F3-04, size reservation batches to the movement limit and allow several reservation batches in flight. State the R6 arithmetic |
| F3-20 | Minor | §3.3 put; item identifier (exporter, keyed digest) | Identical non-conforming payloads received on different days share one item, so the later receipt's payload expires at the first's bucket expiry. The log names the bucket the put actually used |

## Decisions taken

| # | Decision | Balance |
|---|---|---|
| D-19-1 | A plain cancellation fee is charged only on a cancellation. A shorten to a later end charges only a minimum-term fee whose term the new end falls inside | Buyers (incl. agents shortening programmatically) are not surprised. The partner's commitment ends only on a cancellation. Share's risk is unchanged (the fee was in the hold) |
| D-19-2 | A window ending inside a shown minimum term: meanwhile a Share-borne fee line (M11) | The buyer is never charged against P3-N12. The partner is paid on the ledger. The cost is small and rare, and RQ-19-2 lets the requirements owner move it to the buyer |
| D-19-3 | The dearer-path difference is computed against the buyer's charge before corrections of the buyer's price | Suppliers are paid the path's value once. Share never pays twice |
| D-19-4 | Downward cost-line amendments post nothing for cause Share or "other". For cause supplier, they are that supplier's own reduction as debt | Keeps POL-23 "earnings final" and "the party whose device erred bears it" |
| D-19-5 | A reservation price correction applies as `min(corrected, effective price)` and is validated against the effective price | A correction can never raise a minute's charge. Finance can still correct an open-ended re-price downward |
| D-19-6 | The LB price span is `[now − 310 s, now + 60 s]` (at the defaults), computed once, by RFC-004 | Exposure is never understated after a price decrease. The cost is at most 310 s of the old price, once per change |
| D-19-7 | Finance-attributed parts are excluded from attribution-lag breaches and the X2-S22 measure | The breach feed stays meaningful for risk. Provisioning lag is still reported |
| D-19-8 | After a move, "its price" keeps following the original reservation's lock and its notified changes. The path price is the one shown in the offer, fixed for the reservation's life | The buyer keeps the lower of two known prices (POL-07 step 4). Share never profits from the move |
