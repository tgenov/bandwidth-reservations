## Verdict

**REJECT.** I found 2 Blockers, 11 Majors and 17 Minors (30 findings). Both Blockers can
be fixed inside this contract, each with a one-line erratum to its source RFC, so a fix
verification should be enough.

- **R37-01 (Blocker).** The credit-limit command deduplicates *after* the envelope's
  freshness checks. A retry of an F5 that was applied but whose answer was lost is
  therefore answered `refused{unauthorised}`. The console then records an applied credit
  limit as refused.
- **R37-02 (Blocker).** The payout instruction's amount `K = floor_minor(pay × rate)` uses
  `pay`, and RFC-004 §B7.2 then adds "every FailedFinal lot" to a new lot of
  `round_down(pay)`, although `pay` already contains those lots. P4-S86 instructs 240 USD
  to a supplier owed 120 USD.

The settled decisions are applied consistently, with one exception. S1 removes the
membership check at version v but leaves the console's fan-out list undefined (R37-11).
The errata list is incomplete (R37-13). S2 (refused under a recovery hold) is correct
against `requirements-v1.10` X1-S46 and POL-32. RFC-000 rev. 4.4 predicted "held", but
v1.10 settled "refused", and RFC-000 needs more errata than the §8 row alone.

## Decisions taken

| # | Decision | Balance |
|---|---|---|
| D-37-1 | **Reversals**: at most one reversal per provider transaction, for at most the bound amount and in the bound currency, converted at the bound conversion (a full reversal debits exactly the micros credited; a partial one `floor_µ(µ_bound × part / amount)`). Anything else gets `mismatch_recorded` and goes to reconciliation | Buyers are never debited more than they were credited. Share's exposure to odd provider behaviour is moved to reconciliation, not to the wallet. Easy to operate |
| D-37-2 | **Quote validity** is judged by the provider-signed `occurred_at`, if received within 24 h of the quote's expiry | Buyers (and AI agents retrying top-ups) are not penalised for Share's own 503s. The FX risk over hours is small and goes to Share's FX account under POL-20 |
| D-37-3 | **Unknown `account_ref`** gets `accepted{unattributed}`: the binding is recorded, nothing moves, it is escalated to finance and resolved by M22 | The provider stops retrying. The money is traceable, not stranded, and reconciliation matches it once resolved |
| D-37-4 | **No payout destination yet**: lots are carried forward without escalation, every supplier administrator is told each run, and it is escalated after 4 runs | Suppliers are prompted, and finance is not paged weekly for onboarding gaps |
| D-37-5 | **Late `CreditLimitRemoved`** is superseded by any F5 *set* committed after its `decided_at` (R37-03) | Finance's later decision wins, which is P4-S83's intent ("until a finance operator sets one again") |
| D-37-6 | **Payout-provider certification** (K6) requires an idempotency retention of at least 8 days | Prevents double execution without new machinery |
