# 38. Contract review: C12 partner port, partner driver, partner reconciliation feed, equivalent-path source

Date: 2026-10-12. Mode: contract. Focus: concurrency and money.
Under review: `docs/04-delivery/contracts/C12-partner-port.md` version 1.0 (candidate for freeze).
Authority: `requirements-v1.10` (policy register, glossary, P1/P2 features); RFC-000 revision 4.4
§5.2 and §8 (rows "Partner port", "Partner reconciliation feed", "Equivalent-path source",
"Outcome and expiry port"); RFC-002 revision 5 §1.3, §5.5, §9.3–§9.7, §10, §12.1, §13 (contract
sections only); RFC-005 §8.2.

Tools: `tools/check_specs.py`: no problems (473 scenarios). `tools/impact.py coverage` has no
C12 slice; every P1/P2 ID cited in C12 exists at v1.10 (checked by `rg`). Traceability gaps
are in m10.

## Verdict

**ACCEPT WITH CHANGES.** There are no Blockers and 14 Majors. Most of the schema is sound,
and the main safety mechanisms are right: request-scoped release and tombstone, the
fence-checked J2/J3 send time, `expires_at = D`, key-based partner idempotency, and
cancel-at-Share's-cost for residue. The defects cluster in four places:

- what happens after a partner leg is booked: changes, cancels, several bookings per leg,
  and deferred effects;
- the settlement S1, which conflicts with POL-08 for shortening, and whose list of errata
  is incomplete;
- fencing of stale senders and stale adapters, and when an "absent" answer may be trusted;
- reconciliation races, and a money gap in `TERMS_DIFFER`.

All of them can be fixed inside the contract, plus RFC errata. None needs a redesign. C12
should not be frozen until F1–F14 are applied.

## Decisions taken

- **D1 (F1).** A shorten over a partner that cannot amend is accepted. It is carried out by a
  cancel at the partner, deferred to `newEnd`. The buyer pays only a minimum-term fee shown
  at booking that the new end falls inside. Share bears any plain cancellation fee the
  partner charges for this cancel. *Reasoning:* buyers, including agents, keep POL-08's
  unconditional right to shorten. Suppliers get what their terms say. Share's exposure is
  limited to plain fees at partners that cannot amend, and commercial can price that in at
  listing.
- **D2 (F6).** Residue cancellation (POL-28) is the adapter's alone, triggered by `release`
  or a tombstone. This keeps a single writer, matches V-03, and leaves the owner with no
  cost decision to make for residues.
- **D3 (F7).** The stale-sender high-water is kept per account partition, as RFC-000 §5.2
  says. The per-request J7 barrier stays as well. `release` is exempt.
- **D4 (F13).** Reconciliation never acts on a ref that has work in flight. PARTNER_ONLY
  actions are idempotent per ref across days.
- **D5 (F14).** A partner shortfall found by `TERMS_DIFFER` is treated as a partial
  SHARE_ONLY: the leg is marked affected for the missing capacity, and the buyer is not
  charged for it.
- **D6 (F11).** `σ_p_max` = 500 ms (est.), and the granularity bound above, are added to
  the *with holds* listing condition. A partner that fails them is booked as *without
  holds*.
