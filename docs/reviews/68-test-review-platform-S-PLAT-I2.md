## Verdict

**INADEQUATE.** 3 Blocker, 14 Major, 6 Minor.

The codec, checkpoint, timing-alarm and S3 tests are strong: exact codes, exact values, boundaries at 48 h, 5 min, 1 min and the τ cap. The runtime tests are weaker. Most runtime tests also use the wall clock and sleeps (TR-3).
