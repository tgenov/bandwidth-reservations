## Verification

**Verdict: ACCEPT WITH CHANGES.** Revision 6 fixes all 18 findings and applies D1 … D4.
Review 27's Blockers do not regress, and both RFC-005 revision 5 points are correct. Two
Minors and two nits remain, all in the fixes of R33-01, R33-02, R33-03 and R33-10. **Each
one is a precise local edit of a few lines, so the orchestrator may apply them directly
without another round.**

### Defects

| # | Severity | Location | Failure | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V-1 | Minor (local edit) | §2 "a signed verdict per region: 'region X passing' if its last 3 canaries passed", and condition (b); DT-40 | The verdict does not say whether X **announces**, but "the last announcing region is never withdrawn" (review 27 D3) depends on it. **Trace**: R1 has been withdrawn and recovers at `t₀`. The probe sites report R1 passing from about `t₀` + 6 s (3 unicast canaries). R1 re-announces only at `t₀` + 10 s (10 s of its own canaries). If R2's edges fail three canaries in that window (a takeover gap, or a failback step), R2 sees a majority report R1 passing and withdraws. Both prefixes are then down for up to about 4 s plus BGP convergence: a black hole | Make the verdict "region X **announcing and passing**". Each region's edges state their announcement in the signed answer to the probe site's unicast canary, and the probe site includes it. A region with a withdrawn prefix is never reported as a reason to withdraw the other. Add to DT-40: "while a recovered region has not yet re-announced, the other region does not withdraw" |
| V-2 | Minor (local edit; bounded by the 20 s fallback) | §7.3 step 4 ("through the active controller (kept in the preferred region and reachable from both sides)"; "reorders … so that the chosen facility's replica, then R2's, come first, and triggers preferred leader election …; a partition whose chosen-facility replica is out of the in-sync set elects R2's"; "the cut-off facility's replicas leave the in-sync sets"); §5.6 metro-split row | Two premises are false. (a) Kafka's preferred election elects **only the first** assigned replica, and only if it is in the in-sync set. Otherwise it fails (`PREFERRED_LEADER_NOT_AVAILABLE`); it does not fall back to the second. For a partition led in the cut-off facility, the chosen facility's follower cannot fetch, so it leaves the in-sync set within 4 s (`replica.lag.time.max.ms`), before placement acts (state at about 4 s). The election therefore fails for nearly every such partition. For the same reason it is the **chosen** facility's replicas that leave those partitions' in-sync sets, not the cut-off facility's. (b) The active controller is in one of R1's facilities (§5.2), so it is directly reachable from one side only. If it is in the cut-off facility, the chosen side's brokers miss their heartbeats and are **fenced** after 4 s (`broker.session.timeout.ms`), and the leaders of chosen-led partitions can move to cut-off replicas. Only the 20 s controlled shutdown then restores service, with every leader in R2 | Rewrite step 4: (1) placement issues admin calls through an R2 broker. (2) If the active controller is in the cut-off facility, placement makes a controlled restart of that controller through an R2 path, and §5.2's preferred-region restart rule is suspended for the split's duration, so a chosen-side or R2 controller is accepted. With the controller on the chosen side or in R2, the cut-off brokers that cannot heartbeat are fenced by Kafka itself. (3) For each partition still led in the cut-off facility, put **its first in-sync replica among R2's** first and elect. Once the chosen facility's replica is back in sync, put it first and elect again. (4) Keep the 20 s controlled-shutdown fallback. Correct "the cut-off facility's replicas leave the in-sync sets" to "the replicas that cannot reach their leader leave the in-sync sets". Mirror this in the §5.6 row |
| V-3 | Nit | §4.2 re-home: "(step 1, reading the new session row so `T_res` is above the re-home's commit)" | The re-home transaction does not write the new session row; the session is opened beforehand. What lifts `T_res` above the re-home's commit is the read of the re-homed `owner_slot` rows, which step 1 also makes. The argument holds, but its justification names the wrong row | "(step 1, whose read of the re-homed `owner_slot` rows, written by the re-home, puts `T_res` above the re-home's commit)" |
| V-4 | Nit | §5.6 correlated-shrink row ("produce continues within about 3 s"); §22.1 row "R2's fetch stalled for 6 s … Produce continues within 3 s" | The time origin is unstated. From the stall's start, the in-sync shrink takes 4 s (`acks=all` stalls meanwhile), the trigger 2 s more, then the admin call: about 6 to 7 s, at the §10.3 breach-free bound of 7 s. A 6 s stall ends just as the trigger fires, so the spike row tests almost nothing | State "within about 3 s of the in-sync shrink (about 7 s from the stall's start, at §10.3's 7 s bound)". Make the spike row a stall of 15 s with the pass condition "produce resumes within 7 s of the stall's start" |
