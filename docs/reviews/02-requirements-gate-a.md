## Verdict

**FAIL.** The rewrite since review 01 is substantial and mostly sound. Verification metadata
is gone, the prototype mechanics are out of P2, every policy parameter is named, the
arithmetic in every Examples table is correct, and need traceability is complete in both
directions. But the baseline still contradicts itself on the one guarantee the business
says it has settled (C4: competing buyers never all lose). P2-S15 and P2-N4 promise it
without condition, while P2-S18 (`wait = false`), buyer-chosen deadlines and partner
failures each allow every competitor to lose. And the default priority rule (P2-S16,
`request_age`) cannot be met together with the waiting rule (POL-05, P2-S52) when the
younger request holds the capacity first. Both are policy contradictions, so no RFC can
satisfy the specs as written. There are also major gaps. The lifecycle has no funds,
concurrency or partner failure cases. Concurrent spending by one account or agent key is
not covered, nor are an agent key's power over its own limits, a supplier's authority over
its own link, the effect of a capacity loss on pending requests, the terms of a permanent
reduction, or an operator's power to cancel. Answer the two Blocker policy questions, then
the five Major ones, and apply the fixes. After that this scope can pass.

## Policy questions (verbatim, for the business)

- **GA-01.** Over which requests does the "never all lose" invariant hold? Options: (a) only over requests that wait and whose deadline outlasts the outcome of the request ahead of them; requests that opt out (`wait = false`) or choose a shorter deadline are excluded by their own choice; (b) unconditionally: a request rejected under `wait = false`, or whose deadline expires while it waits, keeps its claim and is confirmed if the capacity is released within some bound; (c) unconditionally, by refusing `wait = false` and short deadlines when capacity is in flight (contradicts POL-05).
- **GA-02.** Does `priority_rule` let an older request take capacity that a younger in-flight request already holds, or does it only order requests that are waiting? Options: (a) it only orders waiters and breaks crossings; capacity already held goes to the holder; (b) an older request always takes precedence over a younger hold (pre-emption), and the younger goes back to waiting within its deadline; (c) as (b), but the pre-empted request is rejected with a counter-offer. Also: does a request that loses a crossing wait or get rejected?
- **GA-04.** May a request that was still being committed at its deadline be confirmed after it, within `commit_margin`? Options: (a) no; every outcome exists at the deadline, and `commit_margin` is only a safety allowance and the alert threshold; (b) yes; the buyer-visible bound is deadline + `commit_margin`; (c) yes, but only if the commit began before the deadline, and that is visible to the buyer as a distinct reason.
- **GA-08.** What are the mechanics of a permanent reduction? (1) Offer to every affected buyer, or only to those who would otherwise be revoked? (2) Revoke only as many, newest first, as needed to fit, or every reservation not re-routed? (3) Must the supplier keep the capacity for `revocation_notice`, or is the reduction immediate, with the reservations to be revoked "affected" until revocation? (4) Does an offer lapse after an `offer_window`, and does no answer count as declined or accepted? (5) Is "newest" the original confirmation, or does each added part have its own age?
- **GA-09.** May an operator end a buyer's reservation other than at the account's request or under POL-07/POL-16? Options: (a) only at the account's documented request; (b) also for a closed list of reasons (fraud, sanctions or legal order, abuse), with notice and charge terms; (c) never.
- **GA-17.** When an agent key is revoked, what happens to its requests still pending? Options: (a) they continue to their outcome; (b) they are failed with the reason "key revoked" and release what they hold; (c) the account chooses at revocation.
- **GA-21.** Which money counts as a key's spending in a `key_spend_period`? Options: (a) charges accrued in the period by any reservation the key booked, whenever booked, plus funds currently held for those reservations' future charges, without double counting; (b) only new commitments in the period: the full coverage-window hold of each booking or change made in it; (c) only charges posted in the period, with holds counted against account headroom only.
- **GA-22.** What bounds requests and capacity in flight above the key? Options: (a) an account-level `account_rate_limit` across all keys and users, plus a cap on keys per account; (b) (a) plus a per-link cap on one account's share of capacity in flight; (c) rely on KYC tiers to bound accounts per actor; (d) accept and record the risk. Also: do status polls and retries count toward the limit?
- **GA-23.** Should idempotency keys be scoped per account or per principal (agent key)? Options: (a) per account, as now; (b) per agent key and per account user; (c) per account, but a reuse by a different principal is refused rather than returning the other principal's outcome.
- **GA-24.** How long does an idempotency key identify its request, and which fields define "the same request"? Options: (a) a fixed `idempotency_retention` (for example 24 h); (b) the life of the reservation plus history retention; (c) forever. Sameness: (i) every field sent; (ii) route, window, rate and product only.
- **GA-25.** When does a rate increase take effect, and can it cover only part of the remaining window? Options: (a) from the next minute boundary to the end only; (b) from any future minute boundary the buyer chooses, to the end; (c) any sub-window, making the reserved rate vary over time.
- **GA-26.** Is capacity in flight counted per request (the reserved rate) or per leg (rate × legs)? Options: (a) per request; (b) per leg; (c) per leg on Share links only.
- **GA-27.** May a buyer withdraw a pending request? Options: (a) yes; it fails as "withdrawn" and releases what it holds (a no-hold partner leg already booked is cancelled at Share's cost); (b) no; it runs to its outcome and may then be cancelled under POL-08.
- **GA-28.** Is a planned maintenance booking charged, and in which calendar are business days counted? Options: (a) never charged, and excluded from rating and settlement; (b) charged to the supplier at the reservation price; (c) charged beyond an allowance. Calendar: (i) UTC weekdays; (ii) the link's local working calendar; (iii) replace with calendar days.
