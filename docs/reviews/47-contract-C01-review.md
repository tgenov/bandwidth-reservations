## Verdict

**REJECT** (2 Blockers, 8 Majors, 21 Minors). The fixes are local: a column on the head
row, a rule for projection keys, and some missing operations and errata rows. None
reopens a settled point (M1 … M13), so a fix verification is enough. No full re-review is
needed.

The two Blockers come from the same gap. The contract fixes how the head row survives a
move (keyed by home, columns change). It does not say the same for the rest of a
partition's rows, and it does not say for the head row whether `adopt` has happened. So
after a move, the partition's past rows are hidden from the new slot's guards (B1). The
new slot can also serve the partition before `adopt`, which skips the reader fence and
the takeover entry (B2).

**Arithmetic checked.**
- Holder's view: `t_send` + 3,000 − 100 = 2,900 ms after the last good renewal. A successor
  acquires no earlier than the renewal's commit + 3,000 − `max_offset` ≥ `t_send` + 2,900 ms.
  This is correct, as long as CockroachDB keeps pairwise offset below `max_offset`. That
  holds only through its self-termination heuristic (a node leaves when its offset to a
  majority exceeds 80% of `max_offset`) and RFC-006 §6's alarm ladder. The read-lease
  safety of P2-S10 relies on this timing, while commit safety does not. C01 should cite
  §6.3 as a precondition (folded into R47-23).
- Read lease: 305 + 400 + 100 = 805 ms ≤ 1 s.
- Takeover: 3.0 s expiry + ≤ 250 ms poll + about 30 ms acquisition + catch-up gives 3.3–3.8 s.
- Outbox: 26 h TTL > 25 h GC > the 25 h gap-fill horizon.

Unmarked estimates in C01: `Moving` "about 100 ms" (§3.2 table; marked est. in RFC-006),
takeover 3.3–3.8 s, and the 4,096 → 8,192 figure (R47-30).

## Decisions taken

| # | Decision | Balance |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Probing faults (X2-S33): one `PROBING_FAULT` alert episode per (region, minute) listing every short-probed slot, severity `warning`. It escalates to `page` only when the same slot is short for 5 consecutive minutes or more than 1% of slots are short in a minute. Every slot-minute is still recorded in probe results | Satisfies "alerted … for slot 17 in that minute" without a 16,384-slot page storm for a small on-call team |
| D2 | An integrity stop is acknowledged to audit only once it is enforced at the partition's slot fence (R47-04). The extra latency (one placement transaction) is accepted | Auditors and Share's risk outweigh the milliseconds; nothing is decided on an altered log after "stopped" |
| D3 | On a feed-event content conflict, the database's committed row wins, and the stream blocks at that `stream_seq` until it is read | Integrity of buyers' and suppliers' facts over a few seconds of feed latency |
| D4 | `STALE_SENDER` with a higher recorded generation is a superseded command, not a lost slot (R47-09) | Availability for buyers (including AI agents polling) during doublings; no safety cost, since the receiver already refused |

## Requirements issues

None blocking. X2-S33 is sound. Its traceability gap is RFC-006's (R47-26), and decision
D1 fixes the alert granularity the scenario leaves open.
