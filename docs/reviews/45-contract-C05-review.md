## Verdict

**ACCEPT WITH CHANGES.** There are no Blockers. Six Majors must be fixed before the contract is Frozen. Two are money-safety gaps in the schema: the price of an increase queued ahead, and the provisional key record. Two are concurrency gaps in identity freshness: the `iamFresh` definition, and the `completeThrough` skip rule. Two are contradictions with sibling contracts C06 and C07, which would freeze two different wire forms for one message or command.

Worked figures were recomputed, and every one is correct (see "Arithmetic").

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Location | Finding | Proposed change |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Major | Schema `QueuedChange`; Clarification C-3; test P4 | **The price of an increase ahead is missing.** The envelope ρ⁺(m) prices every addition ahead as confirmed (RFC-004 §A5.2 items 1 and 3). An increase locks the **current** catalogue price, which can differ from the reservation's parts (P4-S60 row 1: +50 Mbps at 3 µ on a 2 µ reservation). `QueuedChange` carries `addedMbps` and `interval` but no price, and `scheduleBefore` holds only committed parts. Trace: a fixed window at 2 µ; catalogue now 3 µ; I1 (+50 Mbps) queued; extension E2 queued behind it. The true ρ⁺ is 100×2 + 50×3 = 350 µ/min. The module can only derive 300 µ/min, so `x` is 50 µ/min short over E2's minutes. When I1 confirms, E2's decision has `R(after) − R(before) > h_q`. That is a failed roll-forward, and it can suspend the account because of its own queue. This breaks P2-S70, P4-S66 and invariant A-2, and makes conformance test P4 unpassable | Add `lockedPrice: Picos` (and `priceVersion`) to `QueuedChange` for `Increase`, copied from that change's E1. Specify ρ⁺ with each ahead part at max(its locked price, a recorded stepped price in force). Add an ahead-increase-at-a-higher-price case to P4 |
| M2 | Major | Identity-freshness watermark; S-2 | **The `iamFresh` definition drops "applied".** S-2 takes each partition's "latest resolved timestamp". RFC-005 §3.3 ("minimum resolved timestamp **applied**") and C08 §B ("latest resolved timestamp applied") both require the events under it to be applied. Funds inputs buffer `stream_seq` gaps (Delivery table; relay-path rows arrive out of order, RFC-006). Trace: key K is revoked at identity at t0, and its push event sits in a gap buffer. Resolved markers keep arriving, so `iamFresh` keeps advancing. E5s for K's requests keep passing `ts ≤ iamFresh + 45 s` indefinitely, past X1-S06 and POL-25's 60 s. The same hazard applies if a catch-up's read timestamp is adopted before its F4 entries commit. Also unclear: after an acquisition, can a marker from one partition set the watermark? C08 says "until the first marker or catch-up" | Define: "a partition's resolved timestamp R counts only once every event of that partition at or below R has been applied (its F4 committed, or found a no-op), with no `stream_seq` gap below it. A catch-up's read timestamp counts only once the entry applying it has committed (or it had no event). After any acquisition the watermark is set only by the catch-up, or once **every** partition feeding the slot has delivered such a timestamp." Add these cases to H4 |
| M3 | Major | F4 `completeThrough`; apply rule "skips any event at or below `completeThrough`"; Idempotency F4 | **The `id_ts` skip can drop real events.** No precondition fixes what `completeThrough` may be. Events of one account are distinct insert-only rows, and changefeeds order them only up to a resolved timestamp. Suppose an F4 written on arrival records `completeThrough` = an event's own timestamp, or a resolved timestamp with a gap below it. A later-arriving event with a lower commit timestamp (a revocation, or a KYB failure) is then skipped forever. The skip is also redundant: F4 is already idempotent by (identity partition, sequence), with highest-version-wins per key (Idempotency table; C08 "idempotent by `source`") | Preferred: delete the timestamp skip and rely on the (identity partition, sequence) high-water plus version, which is cheaper and exact. Otherwise state: `completeThrough` is a resolved timestamp (push) or read timestamp (catch-up) at or below which every event of the account has been received gap-free. The owner never records a higher one |
| M4 | Major | `ProvisionalKeyRecord`; S-1; apply F4 `revokedKeys` | **The provisional key record is not the signed record.** C08 `SignedKeyRecord` / RFC-005 C1 carry `tenant`, `status`, `limits`, `version: number`, `probe` and an Ed25519 signature over JCS. C05's type lacks `tenant`/`account`, `status` and `probe`, and types `version` and `keyRateLimit` as `bigint`. Consequences: (a) the RFC-004 erratum's "check 0 reads a provisional key" cannot read a status that is absent; (b) the account check and the signature cannot be re-verified from E1 on replay or audit, so a compromised owner or edge could record invented limits undetectably (T-33, DT-25); (c) it is unstated whether a key **first applied as revoked** (created and revoked before its first F4) is listed in `revokedKeys`. If it is not, requests admitted under its provisional record survive the revocation until their deadline | Record C08's `SignedKeyRecord` verbatim (the signed bytes) in E1/E1′, and derive the module's view from it. Refuse a provisional record whose `status` is `revoked` or whose tenant ≠ the account. State that `revokedKeys` lists every key whose applied status becomes revoked, **including from absent**. Add both to H6 |
| M5 | Major | F5 apply rule (set-scoped); S-3 vs C07 | **C05 contradicts C07, the credit-limit wire contract.** (a) C05: the account "is a member of the signed set at `mapVersion`". C07 (its settled S1): v is **not** checked, because that would deadlock new-slot publication, and is recorded only. (b) S-3's answer "temporarily unavailable" is not in C07's `CreditLimitAnswer` union, so the console (RFC-004 §B12, "outcome = list of per-account answers") would record it as final for a set-scoped member. (c) The order is unstated: a repeat `action_id` while the slot is stale should still answer the first outcome (it is read-only), not "temporarily unavailable" | Adopt C07's rule (Decision D-2): drop membership-at-v from C05 and record `setScope.mapVersion` and the designating F9 version. Add `{ outcome: "unavailable" }` to C07's answer, stated as never final, so the console retries. Order: envelope → scope → dedupe → freshness gate → validity → KYB |
| M6 | Major | Kafka messages `account.usage_applied.v1`; `UsageAppliedAck`; Idempotency; gap recovery | **C05 and C06 both define the usage-applied acknowledgement, and they disagree.** Topic: `account.usage_applied.v1` vs `account.usage-applied.v1`. Fields: `ratingPos`/`appliedAt`/`entry` vs `appliedThrough`/`appliedTs`/`inFlightUsageBatch`. Envelope: RFC-006 headers vs an encrypted JSON body with `type`, `schemaVersion` and `eventId`. Dedupe: (partition, stream, `stream_seq`) vs (account, `appliedThrough`). C06 also omits `usage_applied_since`. Rating and the owner would be coded to different contracts | One owner of the wire form (Decision D-5): C06 owns topic, body and encoding. C05 keeps the semantics (one per F2, `appliedTs` = F2's `ts`, none while gated or for probes) and the `usage_applied_since` query, and references C06's type. Add `entry: LogPosition` to C06's body |
| m1 | Minor | F7 `enforcement_check`; F8 rule; Alerts | **Late enforcement is mis-reported.** (a) The F7 fires at exactly `since + 60 s`, so an on-time block acknowledged at +59.5 s but delivered at +60.3 s is reported late to finance. (b) Under fast suspend → resume (stdSeq n, n+1 within seconds), network operations applies only the higher `stdSeq` and never acks n, so n is falsely reported late. (c) The F7 alert and then the F8 alert double-report. (d) The check is armed for resumptions too, though X2-S08 bounds only suspension | Decision D-3 |
| m2 | Minor | `apply` F7 Deferred; F8 rule; P11, P12 | Several comparisons mix `EpochMs` (ms) with `HlcTimestamp` (ns): `instant > ctx.ts`, `enforcedAt > since + 60 s`, and `due` instants derived from `since`. No conversion or rounding is given, so P12's "+60 s exactly / +1 ms" boundary is ambiguous | State `instant ≤ ts` ⇔ `instant × 10⁶ ≤ ts.wallNanos`, and `due` instants = `ceil(since.wallNanos / 10⁶) + 60,000` |
| m3 | Minor | F3; Units | No exact integer formula for `LB` across units (Mbps, ms, pico-USD/GB). Implementations could round at GB or µ and diverge from replay | State `LB = ceil(usage_rate_cap × Λ_ms × hp*_pico / 8×10¹²)` µ. Check: 100 × 430,000 × 10¹⁰ / 8×10¹² = 53,750 ✓ |
| m4 | Minor | `HeadroomAnswer.position.ts`; Headroom query semantics | `position.ts` could be the position's commit timestamp or the evaluation (read) timestamp. P4-S72 needs the read timestamp (`A` at `m_now`, the key-spend period). Also unstated: does `answer` use LB, `H_r` and price steps **as applied** (stepped only by F7 entries) or re-derive them at the read timestamp? | Split into `position.ts` (entry) and `evaluatedAt` (= `ctx.ts`). State that `answer` reads applied state, and that only `A` and the `KP_k` period boundary use `evaluatedAt` |
| m5 | Minor | `HeadroomError` | No `NOT_FOUND`, and no replica-side tenant check (RFC-005 C1 receiver obligation; C2 answers `not_found` outside the tenant). `PERMISSION_DENIED` for suppliers leaks existence | Add `NOT_FOUND` for tenant ≠ account (and for suppliers). State the replica re-checks C1 |
| m6 | Minor | F5 `set` | `iamFreshUsed?` is optional, though S-3 makes it required on `set`. Nothing records what the scope check used | Make it required in the `set` variant. Add `designatedAtMapVersion` (from F9) beside `setScope.mapVersion` (with D-2) |
| m7 | Minor | Consumer obligations | Errata incomplete. S-2 also needs RFC-000 §8 (identity-status row, still "advanced by every push heartbeat's resolved timestamp"), RFC-004 §A4 F4 row and §B12 consumed identity row, and RFC-004 revision-5 item 1. S-1 needs RFC-005 C3 ("a record … with a version lower than the applied one is ignored" implies a newer record overrides the applied key; C05 uses records only while the key is absent). S-3 needs RFC-005 §7.4 / RFC-004 §B12 console fan-out to treat "temporarily unavailable" as no answer. Alignment with C06 (M6) and C07 (M5) is missing | Add these rows |
| m8 | Minor | F4 `source` | C08's catch-up for `since` older than 7 days answers a **snapshot**, applied as the account's full state. F4 has no `snapshot` source and no "absent in snapshot" semantics | Add `source: 'snapshot'` and the rule "keys absent from the snapshot are revoked as applied" (or whatever C08 states) |
| m9 | Minor | Usage-applied acknowledgement; gap recovery | (a) "written in each F2 entry's transaction, after commit" is self-contradictory. (b) Old events are rebuilt from "sealed log segments", but entries between the outbox TTL (26 h, RFC-006) and sealing are in the live table; the rebuild must use the log rows' `emits` (RFC-006 §3) to reproduce `stream_seq`. (c) The 7-day retention is not sized: 25,000–50,000 acks/s (RFC-003 / RFC-004 §A11) × 604,800 s ≈ 1.5–3.0 × 10¹⁰ events; at about 200 B each that is about 3–6 TB per copy and 9–18 TB at replication factor 3 (est.). `RatingPosition.inputs` is unbounded | Fix the wording. Rebuild "from the account log (live rows or sealed segments) by `emits`". Add the sizing (est.) and a size cap on `inputs` |
| m10 | Minor | `usage_applied_since` (S-4) | Over-engineered for its use. Acks are monotone ("a lower `ratingPos` … changes nothing"), and a missing ack only delays the "counted" time of its records to the next ack. Beyond 7 days attribution lag is already breached, so a 7-year owner-served rebuild on the write path buys nothing measurable. S-4 is binding, so this is recorded, not changed | If kept, serve it from the status replicas or history, not the owner's write path. Otherwise bound it to the log tail and let rating mark such records "counted, time unknown" |
| m11 | Minor | `ReservationChanged` precondition | "Except a POL-07 cut **or revocation** at `T_eff`" follows RFC-004 §A4. RFC-002 §2.2 E11 allows only the POL-07 cut. The contract settles a disagreement silently | List it as S-5, or align RFC-002's E11 text by erratum |
| m12 | Minor | Scenarios relied on | P4-S117 (late POL-07 cut: the buyer is credited 600 µ by an amendment arriving in F1; it can raise headroom and resume) is not cited, though RFC-004 erratum 5.1 touches it. RFC-004 itself leaves P4-S117 and P4-S118 uncited (tool) | Cite P4-S117 (F1 apply, standing re-evaluated). Raise the RFC-004 traceability gap |
| m13 | Minor | `Verdict` | E1/E1′ must record "the headroom and key headroom it was checked against" (J-13, RFC-004 §A4). `admit` returns neither, and rule 2 forbids booking from computing amounts | Add `checked: { headroom: Micros; keyHeadroom?: Micros }` to both variants |
| m14 | Minor | F8 | An ack for an unknown or future `stdSeq`, or a lower one after a later transition, is unspecified (a buggy or forged producer; T-20) | Record it and alert, with no state change and no effect on the check of another `stdSeq` |
| m15 | Minor | H4 | H4 does not test S-2's points: a heartbeat on one partition while another lags; a gap-buffered event (M2); unset after `adopt` and re-home | Add these cases |

## Decisions taken

| # | Decision | Balance |
|---|---|---|
| D-1 | An increase ahead carries its locked price, and the envelope prices each ahead part at max(locked, stepped) (M1) | Buyers and agents are never suspended because of their own queue; at most the 60 s excess is held, already accepted by POL-25 |
| D-2 | For set-scoped credit limits, adopt C07's S1: scope = `member` is this account **and** applied F9 `excluded`. The signed v is recorded, not checked (M5) | No new-slot deadlock. Share's risk is unchanged: a buyer account is never `excluded` (placement is its only writer), so it still cannot gain the limit |
| D-3 | Enforcement reporting. The F7 at `since + 60 s` (suspensions only) checks two things. If a later standing transition has already committed, there is no check. If no F8 has been applied, it raises `ENFORCEMENT_ACK_MISSING` to operations. `LATE_ENFORCEMENT` (operations and finance) is raised once, either by the F8 with `enforcedAt > since + 60 s` or by a second F7 at `since + 120 s` if still no F8 has arrived (m1) | Finance sees only confirmed or long-unacknowledged lates. A real miss is still reported within 120 s. Operations sees a missing ack at 60 s |
| D-4 | A provisional record is used only while the key is absent from applied state (C05's S-1, stricter than RFC-005 C3's wording), recorded as C08's signed bytes. A key first applied as revoked is in `revokedKeys` (M4) | For at most about 1 s (the push lag), a limit lowered at identity may be read at its older, higher value. POL-25's 60 s bound already allows this. The signature can be audited |
| D-5 | C06 owns the usage-applied wire form (topic `account.usage-applied.v1`, body schema, `EventId`). C05 owns its semantics and `usage_applied_since` (M6) | One wire definition. Fewest edits: C06 is already consistent with the other C06 topics |
| D-6 | `iamFresh` counts a partition's resolved timestamp only when it is applied and gap-free, and a catch-up only after its entry commits (M2) | Aligns with RFC-005 and C08 wording. This adds no latency in the normal case |
